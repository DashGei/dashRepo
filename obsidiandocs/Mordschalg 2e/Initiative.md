When the group sits down for the session, they should determine what half of the party is group A, and group B for the purposes of combat turns. The easiest way to do this is to go clockwise around the table, if there is an odd number of players then the person in the middle goes to group B.  
The DM decides which monsters are in Monster group A and Monster group B

When combat starts, whichever side initiated goes first in the turn order, then the opposing enemy group, then the next friendly group, then the final enemy group.

# Effect Resolution

Combat has two Effect Resolution points: A and B. Effect Resolution A occurs after Hostiles A, and Effect Resolution B occurs after Hostiles B.

At Effect Resolution A, resolve effects created by creatures in Group A. At Effect Resolution B, resolve effects created by creatures in Group B. For this purpose, an effect's source is the creature that applied it.

An effect takes effect immediately unless stated otherwise. By default, an effect lasts until the next Effect Resolution associated with its source's group.

A **continuous effect** lasts for its specified number of rounds, ending at the first Effect Resolution associated with its source's group after its duration expires.

An effect that allows a creature to make a Save to end it follows a different rule. The affected creature makes the Save during its source's Effect Resolution. On a success, the effect ends. On a failure, it continues until the creature succeeds or its duration expires. Unless stated otherwise, the creature may repeat the Save at each subsequent Effect Resolution associated with the source's group.

At Effect Resolution, resolve ongoing effects, make any required Saves, update durations, and remove effects that end. Effects triggered by a specific event resolve when that event occurs, unless stated otherwise.
