When the group sits down for the session, they should determine what half of the party is group A, and group B for the purposes of combat turns. The easiest way to do this is to go clockwise around the table, if there is an odd number of players then the person in the middle goes to group B.  
The DM decides which monsters are in Monster group A and Monster group B

When combat starts, whichever side initiated goes first in the turn order, then the opposing enemy group, then the next friendly group, then the final enemy group.

1 Player group A

2 Monster group A

3 Player group B

4 Monster group B
