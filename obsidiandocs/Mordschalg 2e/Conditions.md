Conditions get HP bars, when time passes or as a creature fights a condition they simply roll "damage" against the condition, these have simple HP like 37/40 health.

When a creature fights against something like a saving throw, they roll a number of dice equal to their ability modifier, trying to break the HP of the effect. (this might be weird/time consuming for immediate effects/ needs playtesting)

This does however allow things like: You are restrained, you can try and break out yourself or if another creature uses their action they get a 2d6 bonus to breaking the condition.
