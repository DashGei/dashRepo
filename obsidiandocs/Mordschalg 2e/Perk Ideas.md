### Martial Perks

As a turn start action you can sacrifice 15 feet of movement on your turn to gain 1d6 bonus damage and 30 feet bonus range on bows, crossbows, and firearms until the start of your next turn.

**Parry** when attacked with a strike dealing physical damage, you can make a melee strike with their weapon as the target. If your strike deals more damage then theirs, their strike becomes a miss.

*Prerequisite, Parry* **Parry Riposte** If you use **Parry** and reduce an strike to a miss, you can make a melee strike against the attacker as part of the parry.

*Prerequisite, Parry* **Fluid Parrying** If you have 3 or more Stamina your **Parry** reduces the damage you take by your roll, even if the result isn't a miss.

*Prerequisite*,  **Advanced Swordplay** When you use any reaction and either reduce a strike to a miss, or make a strike dealing 2 or more HP, you regain a reaction.

**Heavy Blows** When you deal Major or Severe Blunt damage with a Strike,  you can push the target up to 10 feet away from you.
