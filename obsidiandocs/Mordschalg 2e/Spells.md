# Spellcasting

Magic allows you to shape the world through spells, each formed from a **Formula** and a **Delivery**. The formula determines what the spell does, while the delivery determines which creatures, objects, or areas it can affect. By spending Strain, you can increase a spell's damage, enlarge its area, reach additional targets, or strengthen certain effects.

## Casting a Spell

Casting a spell takes at least 1 Action. You may devote additional Actions to the casting to invest more Strain before the spell takes effect. For every Action you spend casting a spell, you can invest up to 2 Strain in it. The spell resolves when you finish casting.

For every 1 Strain you devote to **damage**, a spell's target takes 1d6 of the spell's damage type. You may instead spend Strain on the spell's **Delivery** or on an effect that allows additional investment. 
Other than damage and delivery, a spell's **effect** requires 1 Strain of investment in order for the effects to take place. 

## Areas and Targets

Some spells affect an individual creature or object, while others affect an area. An area delivery affects everything within its area unless the spell says otherwise. A spell's area must originate from a point you can see within 12 meters, unless its Delivery specifies that it originates from you.

A creature is affected by an area spell when the spell takes effect, when it enters the area, or when it starts its turn there. The spell's formula determines what happens when a creature is affected.

When a spell has an associated Ability Modifier, any creature who does not wish to undergo the effects of the spell makes a Save against the caster's Mental Modifier. On a success the creature is unaffected by the spell, but still takes the damage.

## Duration and Focus

A spell normally lasts until the start of your next turn. At that point, you may spend 1 Strain to maintain Focus, extending the spell's duration by one additional turn. You may continue maintaining Focus on subsequent turns in the same way, or at any point while focusing you can choose to stop instantly.

Taking Major damage or Moderate Mental damage breaks your Focus, immediately ending any spell whose duration depends on it. Regardless, losing Focus causes the spell's effects to immediately disappear.

# Spell Deliveries

A spell's Delivery determines its initial range, shape, and number of targets. You can spend additional Strain to expand the delivery or affect more targets, as shown below. The listed cost is the initial cost of the Delivery; any expansion adds to that cost.

| Delivery | Cost | Increase                    |
| -------- | ---- | --------------------------- |
| Aura     | 1    | +2 per 1m radius            |
| Cone     | 2    | +2 per additional 1m length |
| Cube     | 1    | +1 per additional 1m cube   |
| Imbue    | 0    | +2 per additional target    |
| Line     | 1    | +1 per 2m length            |
| Bolt     | 0    | +2 per additional target    |
| Sphere   | 2    | + 1 per 1m radius           |
| Touch    | 0    | +1 per additional target    |

- **Aura** - A 2m-radius area centered on  you. You can choose to be unaffected.

- **Cone** - A 3-meter cone extending in front of you.

- **Cube** - A 1-meter cube 

- **Imbue** - Targets a weapon held by a willing creature within 12m. A creature struck with the weapon suffers the spell's effects and damage, although the damage dice are reduced by one level.

- **Line** - A 1m-wide, 4m-long, 2m-tall area extending from you.

- **Bolt** - Strike a creature within 12m who is the target.

- **Sphere** - A 2m-radius sphere centered on a point you can see.

- **Touch** - Targets yourself or a creature you can touch within 1m.

## Spell Formulas

**Bless** Targets add 1d4 to Saves and Strikes for the duration.

**Banish** *INS* Targets must use their movement to leave the area.

**Bane** *INS* Targets subtract 1d4 from Saves and Strikes for the duration.

**Bright** *Radiant* Targets shed light for 6m.

**Cantrip** *Force* Targets have Woe when rolling damage for spells.

**Charm** *Numb* *PRE* Targets are Charmed for the duration.

**Cloak** Targets are Invisible for the duration or until they Strike or Cast.

**Command** *PRE* On their turn, targets must either move in a direction, or Strike a creature of your choice once.

**Dark** *Necrotic* The area is filled with Darkness.

**Door** *MIG* Two linked 1m portals appear in the area. A creature who occupies the space of one portal appears next to the other, and a projectile continues from one portal to the next in the same direction it was when entering.

**Dispel** A magic effects are targets and dispelled.

**Dream** *INS* Targets fall unconscious until pushed or damaged.

**Entangle** *Blunt* Targets are Grappled.

**Fear** *Stress* *INS* Targets are Frightened for the duration.

**Faux** Medium, dead, Mortal targets are raised as Zombies under your control.

**Freeze** *Cold* Targets have a -1 to speed for every Strain spent to do so.

**Gale** *Slashing* Choose a direction. The area is difficult terrain when moving against that direction, and creatures spend no movement when moving with it.

**Image** *ACU* Any visual effect of your choice is created in the area.

**Immolate** *Burn* Targets take damage for duration.

**Invigorate** *Special* Target's Stamina is healed equal to damage.

**Melt** *Acid* Targets take one damage die when taking other Physical damage.

**Mist** *Cold* A cloud of heavy fog appears in the area, which is Obscured. 

**Levitate** *MIG* Targets rise to the ceiling of the area, or by 1m.

**Plague** *Toxic* *MIG*  Targets are Poisoned for the duration.

**Quiet** *Numb* Targets make no sound.

**See** Creatures seeing into the area have Blindsight.

**Shock** *Volt* Targets adjacent to other targets take one damage die.

**Slow** *AGI* Targets have Slow for the duration.

**Spine** *Pierce* . The area becomes difficult terrain. For every 1m a target moves through the area, it takes 1d4 damage.

**Static** *Numb* Targets cannot focus.

**Tell** Targets telepathically receive a 25 word message composed by you, and may reply.

**Ward** Targets reduce Major damage to Moderate damage.

**Wave** *Blunt* *MIG* Targets are pushed 2m away from the origin and fall Prone.

**Wither** *Necrotic* Targets take 1 damage die when taking the Attack or Cast action.

# Rituals

Rituals are magical workings that require time, materials, or particular acts to perform. Unlike ordinary spells, they need not follow a common formula or delivery. Each ritual describes what is required to cast it and what happens when it is complete.

A ritual's **Casting Time** describes how long the working takes, while its **Components** describe the materials required. Unless a ritual states otherwise, follow its own instructions for how these materials are used and what effects the ritual produces.

A **Magic Circle** always occupies a 3m cube and is inscribed using Enchanter's Chalk. The chalk is consumed as the circle is drawn.


### Detect Thoughts

A caster can complete this ritual by taking an hour, shaving 1 Gold's worth of enchanter's chalk into a drop of their saliva. For an hour, the caster can then read the mind of any creature who consumes the entire drop as long as they are within 6m of each other.

### Concentrate

A caster can complete this ritual by taking the time to bless a target. The spellcaster must have 3 Favor with one holy god (Sol, Ilmater, Keep, Ea'or, Frey, Bahamut, Helm, Gri), and they must spend 1 silver worth of salt on the casting for every 10 minutes spent casting. The time required and the effect of the ritual depends on the target.

The effect lasts for a number of months equal to the caster's level.

| Target                | Time     | Effect                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1 Liter of water      | 1 minute | The water becomes holy water                                                                     |
| 10 1m-squares of land | 1 minute | An unholy creature takes Minor Psychic and Radiant damage when they start their turn on the land |

### Summoner's Sheath

A caster can complete this ritual by taking an hour, engraving an arcane tattoo into a part of their body. After the ritual, the caster can sheath or draw a weapon with the sigil as a free action, storing it in a pocket dimension.

### Orcish Tattoo

**Casting Time:** 1 hour 
**Components:** 10 GP of gold

The caster tattoos the gold into a creature of their choice. After which that creature can expend one Strain, causing daylight to spill out of the tattoo and brighten a 6m radius around them until they choose to turn it off.

### Lightning Rod

A caster can inlay 3GP liquid copper into any stick/rod/handle of any material. Afterwards under an open sky the caster can use an Action and 3 Strain causing lighting to leap onto the stick and explode. Creatures within 2m of the rod make a Mental DT DEX Save taking 3d6 Lighting damage on a failed save and half that on a success. If the rod is destroyed as part of the Action the spell deals 3d8 damage.

### Dream Message

The caster sits in a drawn circle with 20 GP of Enchanter's Chalk and sets a bowl of 5 SP incense. The caster then mentally chooses a creature they can visualize fairly accurately. If that creature is asleep then the creature's consciousness drifts to the circle and can only see within the area of the circle. Both creatures can communicate, until the target wakes up naturally, or the caster ends the spell.

### Antithances

The caster sits in a drawn circle with 50 GP of Enchanter's Chalk and deeply bleeds one of their palms onto the circle. The caster then mentally chooses a creature they can visualize fairly accurately. The caster is then able to remotely view through the targets senses for the duration of the spell (Which can be ended at any time). The caster can also choose to take on 1d4 Exhaustion and spend 5  Strain in order to take an action through the target who then becomes aware of the control. Otherwise a creature with more than 2 Instinct or Understanding automatically knows they are being viewed.

For every minute the spell is active, the caster takes 1d10 Vitality damage against the caster's PD which is set to 3 for the duration of the spell. 

### Fireline

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Components:** Powdered coal.

The caster spreads the mixture in a continuous line (1 SP of coal powder per meter) along the ground. The caster can ignite the line as an Action while within 12m, the line becomes a blazing wall 5m high and 3m thick that lasts for 1 minute. A creature that enters the wall or starts its turn there makes a Mental DT DEX Save taking 3d6 Burn damage on a failed save and half that on a success. Once ignited, dispersed, or the powder contaminated by water, the line cannot be reused.

At the start of the ritual the caster drinks the poison. The caster then chooses one creature they can see within 12m. The caster knows whenever the creature knowingly speaks a falsehood to them while within 6m for the next 10 minutes. ### Lesser Ward

**Casting Time:** 30 minutes  
**Components:** Silvered thread.

The caster lines an area with silvered thread (1 SP per 3m). During casting, the caster may designate any number of creatures present as permitted to cross without triggering the ward. Whenever another creature crosses the marked opening, the caster receives a startling mental alarm.

### Gri's Nail

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Components:** Two iron nails.

The caster names a location they have visited and drives the nail into the ground during the ritual. For the next 7 days, a creature holding another nail flat in its palm can cause it to point toward the designated location. The ritual functions only when the caster and destination are on the same plane. The nail indicates direction but not distance or the safest route.

### Speak With Dead

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** 1 SP of honey and 10 GP incense.

The caster sets the incense, drinks the honey, and touches the remains of a creature capable of speech in life. For the next 10 minutes, the corpse can answer up to three questions in a language it knew when alive. It answers truthfully to the best of its knowledge at the time of death, in brief phrases. It cannot answer questions about events that occurred after its death or about information it never knew. A given set of remains cannot be affected by this ritual again for 7 days.

### Purification

**Casting Time:** 1 minute  
**Components:** Silver salt worth 1 SP.

The caster purifies up to 10L of liquid or enough food for six creatures. The ritual removes mundane dirt, contaminants, and spoilage, rendering the food or liquid safe for consumption. It does not neutralize poisons, cure diseases, remove curses, or dispel magical contamination.

### Spectral Friend

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** A Minor iron object and 1 GP of powdered gem.

The caster inlays the powdered gem into the object, which is then bestowed with a colored flame. The flame has no temperature, and can mold itself into any small manipulative device which can hold one Minor item. It will naturally float and follow the caster unless told to roam an area.

A friend has 1 Vitality, a PD of 5, and 1 MIG and shares its creator's Initiative. As an Action the caster can set one friend to get to work and do one of the following:

 - Inscribe a magic circle using up Enchanter's Chalk. It can write 5 1m-squares of circle per Action.
 - Write words spoken by the caster, or continuously write a message or signature.
 - Do a very basic task, such as press a button,  pour a drink, .

Once the task is completed it either flies back to the caster or roams the area.

### Iron Guest

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** A Major iron vessel, and black thread.

The caster can bind an ambient spirit, or the spirit of a dead being with less than 1 Presence in life. An unwilling Spirit makes a Mental DT Presence save to resist capture. 

The servant can project itself as a magical energy within 1m of the vessel and obeys simple commands. It performs repetitive tasks such as carrying objects, fetching items, cleaning, lighting a fire, or opening unlocked doors. It can carry up to 10kg and cannot move more than 12m from the vessel. The servant cannot attack, make Checks, use tools, activate magic items, manipulate the vessel, nor objects held by an unwilling creature. If the vessel is broken the spirit is released.

### Hearth Without Smoke

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** Coal from a previously lit fire and salt worth 1 GP.

The caster establishes a 3m-radius area around a fire, hearth, or point on the ground. Until the next dawn, the ground within the area remains dry, and ordinary wind and rain do not disturb the occupants or extinguish fires within it. The temperature remains comfortable for resting, and fires within the area produce no smoke. A Rest Test made by a party resting within the area uses a Rest Quality Die one step higher than the campsite would normally provide, to a maximum of d12. The ritual does not prevent creatures from entering the area or protect occupants from other hazards.

The caster inscribes matching sigils at two fixed doorways on the same plane. The first sigil remains dormant for up to 30 days while the caster completes the ritual at the second location. Once both sigils are complete, the doorways become linked for 7 days.

### Rimeweillian Lance Mound

**Casting Time** 1 Minute
**Components** 4 GP Prism and 1 GP Enchanter's Chalk

The caster inscribes a magical circle on the ground which helps suspend the prism at head level. While standing in the middle of the circle, Bolt deliveries have a range of 30m, and the caster rolls the Strike with Weal when targeting only one creature.

### Beast Speech

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Components:** A beast's tongue and 1 GP of raw meat.

The caster smears the meat across their tongue and consumes it. For the next hour, the caster can speak with and understand ordinary animals. Animals are not compelled to cooperate, and their ability to communicate is limited by their intelligence and experiences. They cannot describe things they could not perceive through their ordinary senses.

### Animate Dead

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** A corpse and 5 GP of black salt and Enchanter's Chalk per corpse.

The caster marks the corpse with a magic circle and binds its remains to a fragment of the caster's own vitality. The corpse rises as a Zombie under the caster's control. The caster may animate up to three Medium or smaller corpses during the ritual.

The undead remain animated indefinitely, but obey the caster for 24 hours. The caster can mentally command undead created by this ritual within 30m. Undead outside this distance continue their last instruction. The caster may renew control over an undead creature by spending 10 minutes repeating the ritual on it, consuming 1 GP of black salt and Enchanter's Chalk.

If control expires, the undead remains animated but no longer obeys the caster.

### Revivify

**Casting Time:** 1 minute  
**Components:** Powdered diamond worth 50 GP and a drop of the caster's blood.

The caster draws a circle of powdered diamond around a dead creature and performs a rite to bind its departing spirit to its remains. The ritual must begin within 1 minute of the creature's death and must be completed within 10 minutes of its death. The creature must have an intact enough body for its spirit to return.

When the ritual is completed, the creature returns to life with 1 Vitality and 0 Stamina. It retains its injuries and any conditions it suffered before death. The ritual cannot restore missing body parts or return a creature that died of old age.

### Cure Wounds

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Components:** Silver salt worth 5 GP, medicinal herbs, and clean bandages.

The caster binds the target's wounds with the prepared materials and recites a restorative rite. The target regains all missing Stamina and 1 Vitality. This ritual cannot restore missing body parts or remove curses, diseases, poisons, or other conditions that caused the injury. A creature can benefit from Cure Wounds only once between Major Rests.

### Witchlight

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Components:** Powdered gem worth 1 GP per object.

The caster inlays the powdered gem into up to three small objects, each of which gains a dormant colored flame. The flame produces no heat and sheds Bright light within 6m when active.

As an Action, the caster can activate or extinguish any number of prepared lights within 30m that they can see.

### Glyph of Warding

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** Enchanter's Chalk worth 10 GP, plus any material components required by the stored spell.

The caster inscribes a magical glyph on a surface or object no larger than a 1m square. As part of the ritual, the caster inscribes one spell Formula and Delivery into the glyph and pays the spell's normal Strain cost. The caster also defines a simple condition that triggers the glyph, such as a creature crossing a threshold or touching the marked object.

When the condition is met, the glyph releases the stored spell, using the glyph as the point of origin for area effects or the triggering creature as the target when appropriate. All choices required by the spell must be made when the glyph is inscribed. The stored spell resolves using its normal rules, including its Duration.

The glyph remains until triggered, dispelled, or destroyed. A glyph can release its stored spell only once.

### Control Weather

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** Ash, salt, feathers and 10 GP Incense.

The caster prepares a vessel containing the materials and uses it to alter the weather within a 30m radius of a point they can see within 30m. The caster may cause or end ordinary local conditions such as fog, light rain, snowfall, or wind, or adjust the intensity of existing precipitation and wind within what is natural for the region and season.

The altered weather lasts for 1 hour, after which it gradually returns to its natural state. The ritual cannot create severe storms, extreme temperatures, or other conditions that would be impossible under the prevailing climate.

The caster may spend additional Strain when completing the ritual. Each Strain spent increases the radius by 30m, to a maximum radius of 300m.

### Greater Control Weather

**Casting Time:** 24 hours  
**Components:** Weather-appropriate materials and offerings worth 100 GP, including a vessel of stormwater and the blood of a large animal.

The caster performs the ritual in the open air, altering the weather within a 3km radius of a point they can see. The caster may change the wind, precipitation, temperature, and cloud cover, creating conditions such as a heavy storm, dense fog, a blizzard, or an extended period of clear skies, provided the resulting weather is possible within the region and season.

The new weather develops over the course of 1 hour and persists for 24 hours, after which it gradually returns to its natural state. The ritual cannot create a tornado, summon a lightning strike against a chosen creature, or otherwise produce an instantaneous, precisely targeted weather effect.

Upon completing the ritual, the caster loses 1 Vitality. This loss cannot be prevented.

### Speak with Plants

**Casting Time:** 30 minutes  
**Components:** A living root and silver water worth 1 GP.

The caster buries the root in the ground and waters it with the prepared silver water. For the next hour, the caster can speak with and understand ordinary plants within 6m. Plants can communicate impressions of their surroundings and describe events they have perceived, according to their limited senses.

Plants can answer questions about creatures that passed nearby, changes in the weather, disturbances in the soil, and other events they could have perceived. They cannot communicate information beyond their awareness, and the ritual does not grant the caster control over plants or allow them to move beyond their natural capabilities.

### Mirror Link

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** Two mirrors and silver thread worth 10 GP.

The caster carves a symbol into a mirror, which is then linked to any other mirror which bears the same symbol.

When the caster looks into either mirror, they can choose to see the space immediately before the other mirror instead of their own reflection. The image conveys sight and sound, and the caster can observe only what would be visible from the other mirror's position. A viewer can return the mirror to its ordinary reflection and back again at any time by tapping thrice on the mirror.

Deics and Cryptids can spend 5 Strain to move through the mirror. When this happens roll 1d4, on a 4 the mirror shatters after they pass through.

### Far Mirror

**Casting Time:** 1 hour  
**Components:** A polished mirror of obsidian, and powdered onyx worth 500 GP.

The caster performs the ritual before the mirror, describing a specific location on the same plane. The mirror displays the chosen location as though the caster were looking from a point within it. The description must be sufficient to distinguish the location from other places; a vague description or a location that does not exist produces no image.

The mirror conveys sight and sound, and the caster cannot follow a creature beyond the visible area or see through a barrier that would prevent the chosen viewpoint from seeing into the space. The caster can end the vision at any time.

Around the viewport in the viewed space,, the temperature drops by 20 degrees F almost immediately.
