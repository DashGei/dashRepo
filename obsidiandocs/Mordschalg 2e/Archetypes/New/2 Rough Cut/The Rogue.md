## Physical and Mental Modifiers

**Agility** is your Physical Virtue. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct** or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Warlock features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health

A Rogue begins with 3 Vitality, 1 Flesh, 8 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training

As a Rogue, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, hand crossbows, light crossbows, scimitars, rapiers, shortswords
**Skills:** Choose four from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth

## Perks

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stance

At level one, you gain access to a Stance of your choosing. While active, you have all features of that Stance. At later levels you will gain new Stances which you may swap between on a Minor Rest. Losing the old features and gaining the new until you choose to swap back to that Stance.

Any perk you gain which requires that Stance is only ever active while that Stance is active.

## Sneak Attack: 1

Once per turn, you can deal extra damage to one creature you Strike if you have Weal on the Strike. 

The extra damage starts at 2d4 and increases as shown in the Sneak Strike column of the Rogue table. The damage type is the same as the weapon's type.

## Quick Feet: 2

When you take the **Dash Move**, you gain an additional 3m Speed. Also, you can take the **Hide** and **Disengage** Moves as one Action

## Opportunist: 5

When a creature within 6m of you rolls Miss, you can Strike that creature as a Reaction.

## Exploit: 6

When you Strike as part of a Reaction, the Strike is always a Sneak Attack and does not count towards your per turn limit of Sneak Strikes.

## Evasion: 9

When you roll Major on an AGI Save, you can move up to your speed and make one Strike as a Reaction.

## Quick Feet II: 10

When you take any **Reaction**, you can move up to your speed as part of that reaction.

## Adroitness: 11

Whenever you make a Check you have Training in, you roll 2d20 and use the higher roll for the Check.

## Blindside: 14

When you Sneak Strike a creature, you can spend 3 Strain to remove their Reaction.

## Uncanny Dodge: 15

When an attacker that you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to halve the attack's damage against you.

## Slippery: 17

Whenever you make a Save you have Training in, you roll 2d20 and use the higher roll for the Save.

## Dirty Tactics: 18

When you use Blindside to remove a Reaction, you can spend 1 more Strain to gain a Reaction.

## Elusive: 20

No Strike has Weal against you.
