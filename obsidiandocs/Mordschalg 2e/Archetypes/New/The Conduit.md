To be a Conduit is to draw hushed whispers from passersby. Your very essence is likely an affront to at least one god's teachings, and the actions of those who precede you can be devastating. So keep your head, if not for your own sake, then for those who look upon you.

Unlike other spellcasters a Conduit's relationship with their powers is tenuous. Some make bargains with incredible beings and some are unwilling, their powers and charge bestowed upon them by blood or speculative proclamations that they are indeed the chosen.

## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Choose **Might** or **Agility** to determine your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** to determine your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Conduit features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Spellcasting: 0

At 1st level, you learn three Spell Formulas and one Ritual of your choice. You learn additional formulas and rituals as specified by your class features.

You cast spells by combining a Formula with a Delivery. The Formula determines the spell's effect, while the Delivery determines its targets or area. Casting takes at least 1 Action, and for each Action you devote to casting, you may invest up to 2 Strain. Each Strain spent on damage deals 1d6 damage of the spell's type, while a spell's effect requires 1 Strain to activate. You may also spend Strain to modify a spell's Delivery. The spell takes effect when you finish casting.

A creature targeted by a spell with an associated Ability Modifier may make a Save against your Mental Modifier to resist its effects. On a success, the creature is unaffected by the spell's effects but still takes its damage.

A spell normally lasts until the start of your next turn. You may spend 1 Strain at that point to maintain Focus, extending the spell for one additional turn. You can continue spending 1 Strain at the start of each subsequent turn to maintain Focus, or end it at any time. Taking Major damage or Moderate Mental damage breaks your Focus, immediately ending the spell.

### Rituals

Rituals are distinct magical workings, each with its own Casting Time, Components, and effects. Unlike spells, rituals need not follow the standard rules for Formula and Delivery. Follow each ritual's description to perform it and resolve its effects. Ritual components are consumed when the ritual specifies.

## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Some abilities and features refer to your **Physical Modifier** or **Mental Modifier**. These are not separate ability scores. Instead, each represents one of your existing Ability Modifiers, allowing you to use your strongest relevant attribute.

Your Physical Modifier is based on either Might or Agility. Your Mental Modifier is based on Acumen, Instinct, or Presence. Your class determines which abilities you may choose from.

Whenever a rule refers to one of these modifiers, use the modifier of the ability you selected. For example, your Mental Defense (MD) is equal to your Mental Modifier.

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Power Unbound: 1

As an Action, you unleash the power within you and become Empowered for 1 Minute. While Empowered, your Mental Modifier increases by 1, and you may invest up to 3 Strain for each Action you spend casting a spell.

When you become Empowered, roll on the Warlock Instability table using the die determined by your current Mental Health. If your Mental Health falls into a lower Instability band while Empowered, roll again using the new die. The new result replaces your previous Instability effect.

## Mind as Kindling: 2

When you cast a spell, you may lose 1 Mental Health to gain 1 additional Strain to invest in that spell. This additional Strain can exceed your normal limit for Strain invested per Action. You may use this feature once per spell.

## Instability: 5

While Empowered, whenever you cast a spell, roll a number of d10 determined by your Mental State: none while Fine, one while Irate, three while Distressed, and four while Miserable.

Whenever a die rolls a 10, its assigned effect occurs. If multiple dice roll a 10, resolve each triggered effect in an order of your choice. An Instability effect can occur only once per casting, even if multiple dice assigned to it roll a 10.

You learn one Instability effect at 5th level and learn another at 10th, 14th, and 17th level. When you finish aa rest, you can change which Instability effect is applied when you roll a 10.

### Magnification

The spell's Delivery doubles in scale. Double the radius of a Sphere or Aura, the length of a Cone or Line, or volume of a Cube.

### Emanation

A 2m-radius Aura of the spell's Formula manifests around you until the start of your next turn. It affects every creature within it except for you.

### Roaming

If the spell creates an area, that area persists for 1 minute, regardless of focus. At the start of each turn you roll a d4. The area then moves 2m in a direction determined by the d4, where rolling a 1 is North, 2 is East, and so on.

### Confluence

Determine a random spell formula of yours. Its effect is also applied to the original spell's targets or area without additional Strain. The additional Formula does not add another damage roll or damage dice. Any associated Saves and other requirements still apply.

### Devastation

For a spell which deals damage, targets take an additional 2d6 damage. You take 1d6 force damage for every Strain you spent on the spell.
