## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Paladin features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health: 0

A Paladin begins with 2 Vitality, 2 Flesh, 9 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training: 0

As a Paladin, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons
**Tools:** None
**Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion.

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Training: 1

As a Paladin, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** All armor, shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion

## Aura of Zeal: 1

You emanate a protective aura that extends **2m** from you in all directions. Enemies within it never have Weal when Striking allied creatures **other than you**.

### Zeal Dice

**Zeal Dice.** You have a pool of **Zeal Dice** that fuel your divine abilities. Your **Zeal Dice** start as d2s, and they improve at higher levels. You can hold a maximum of **4 Zeal Dice** which disappear when you finish a short or long rest.

At the start of each of your turns, you gain one **Zeal Die** for each hostile creature within your aura. You can use these Dice to do two things:

**Divine Smite.** When you Strike, you can expend any number of **Zeal Dice**. The Strike deals additional **Radiant** damage equal to the total rolled.

**Zealous Benediction.** When you Strike, you can expend two **Zeal Dice** to heal a creature within your aura for 1 Stamina or Flesh (including possibly yourself).

## Spellcasting: 2

At 2nd level, you learn three Spell Formulas. You learn additional formulas as specified by your class boons.

You cast spells by combining a Formula with a Delivery. The Formula determines the spell's effect, while the Delivery determines its targets or area. Casting takes at least 1 Action, and for each Action you devote to casting, you may invest up to 2 Strain. Each Strain spent on damage adds 1d4 damage of the spell's type, while a spell's effect requires 2 Strain to activate. You may also spend Strain to modify a spell's Delivery. The spell takes effect when you finish casting.

A spell normally lasts until the start of your next turn. You may spend 1 Strain at that point to maintain Focus, extending the spell for one additional turn. You can continue spending 1 Strain at the start of each subsequent turn to maintain Focus, or end it at any time. Taking Major damage or Moderate Mental damage breaks your Focus, immediately ending the spell.

## Resilient Zeal: 5

Your conviction hardens as you protect others. When an ally within your aura takes damage **from a hostile**, you gain one Zeal Die. This can happen only **once per turn**.

Additionally, when you use Zealous Benediction to heal an ally, you also regain 1 Stamina or Flesh.

## Aura of Protection: 6

While your aura is active, you and friendly creatures within it add your **Mental Modifier** (minimum +1) to all saving throws.

## Abjure Foes: 9

By spending 2 Strain as an **Action**, you target up to 4 creatures that you can see within 12m. Each must succeed on a **Mental DC PRE** saving throw or be **Frightened** of you for 1 minute. While Frightened in this way, a creature cannot take **Reactions**.

## Aura of Courage: 10

You and allies within your aura have Weal on Saves against **Frightened**.

## Consecrated Ground: 11

As an Action, you can cause your aura to emanate from a point you can see within **6m** instead of from yourself. You must **Focus** on this effect as if concentrating on a spell. If your Focus ends, the aura returns to you immediately.

## Seek Out The Profane : 14

By spending 2 Strain as an Action, you can detect celestials, fiends, and undead. For the next 10 minutes, you know the direction of any creature of those types within 12m of you. You also know if there is a place or object within 6m that has been consecrated or desecrated.

## Zeal Overflow: 15

When you would gain a **Zeal Die** and are already at your maximum, your aura increases in size by **1m** until the start of your next turn.

## Bulwark: 17

At the start of your turn, you gain one **Zeal Die** for each friendly creature within your aura. Your maximum number of **Zeal Dice** increases by your **Mental Modifier**.

## Aura Expansion: 18

Your aura radius increases to 6m.

## Righteous Conviction: 20

Spend 5 Strain as two Actions to invert the protective magics which fill your aura, and turn them against your enemies. The effects last until the start of your next turn..

All enemies within your aura **lose any resistances** they have, and take an additional **2d6 Radiant** damage whenever they take damage.
