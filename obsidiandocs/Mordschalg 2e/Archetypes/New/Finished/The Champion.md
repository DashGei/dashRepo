## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Champion features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health: 0

A Champion begins with 2 Vitality, 2 Flesh, 9 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training

As a Champion, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** All armor, shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Skills:** Choose two from Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, and Survival

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stances: 1

At level 1, you gain access to two Stances of your choice. You can have only one Stance active at a time. As an Action, you can switch to another Stance you know. When you switch, you lose the features of your previous Stance and gain the features of your new Stance. You retain the new Stance's features until you switch again.

A Perk with a Stance prerequisite is active only while its associated Stance is active.

## Technique: 1

As a Champion you can center yourself in combat and choose to enhance certain moves you do, adding a **d8** to their result. You can do so as a **Free Action**, the die is lost until your next group turn.

You can add the result of the **d8** to any of the following:

 - A Strike's damage roll
 - The speed you gain when taking the Dash Action
 - A Save
 - Subtract the result from one damage roll against you.

If you do not use your Technique die before your next group turn, it is lost.

## Power Through: 2

When the party finishes a Rest Test, you may treat its result as **one degree higher** for yourself, to a maximum of Major. The next Rest Test is treated as **one degree lower** for you, to a minimum of Miss.

## Eye for Victory: 5

As an Action, spend 2 Strain and choose up to **4 creatures** you can see within **12m**. You then learn their maximum and current Mental Health.

Using this Action out of combat gives you different information on the chosen creatures. The GM tells you their current disposition to you and eagerness to start combat.

## Overpowering Strike: 6

When you make a Strike, you can choose to replace the enemies PD with their **AGI** Modifier for that Strike. 

## Technique Enhancements: 9

Your Technique die becomes a d10 instead of a d8.

## Battlefield Intuition: 10

When you use Eye for Victory out of combat, the GM also gives you their estimation of how likely your group is to walk away from combat and in what condition. 

You also learn whether additional enemies are present but out of sight, and whether the group has an advantage or tactic you have not accounted for. This feature does not reveal the number of unseen enemies or the nature of any undiscovered advantage.

## All Out Heroics: 11

Once per Major rest when you start your turn, you can choose to gain 2 additional Actions and the benefits of a Moderate Rest. These benefits last for **one minute**, after which you lose all of your Strain, along with 3 Mental Health and Stamina.

## Expert Technique: 14

When your group takes their turn, you can choose to either gain one d10 Technique Die, or two d4 Technique dice to spend individually.

## Power Through II: 15

As a Free Action when you are not **Miserable**, you can sacrifice 1 Mental Health in order to ignore the effects of being **Distressed** or **Irate** for the next minute.

## Disciplined Footwork: 17

When you use an Action to swap between stances, you gain your choice of one d10 or two d4 Technique dice. This allows you to have more Technique dice than normally possible, but you still lose all of them on your next group turn.

## The Equalizer: 20

Your inner strength and wholeness overcomes lesser attempts to alter the reality around you.
You have Weal on Saves against all magical effects, and you are immune to all spell damage where the damage dice are **d6** or lower.
