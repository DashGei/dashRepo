## Physical and Mental Modifiers

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Sovereign features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health

A Sovereign begins with **4 Vitality, 8 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health**.

## Training

As a Sovereign, you have Training in the following areas:

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields  
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons  
**Skills:** Choose three from Athletics, Deception, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, Performance, and Persuasion.

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree: Minor, Moderate, or Major. Perks may also have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose them and how they work.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stance: 1

At 1st level, choose a Stance. While it is active, you gain all features of that Stance. At later levels, you gain additional Stances and may switch between them when you finish a Minor Rest. You lose the features of your previous Stance and gain the features of the new one until you switch again.

A Perk with a Stance prerequisite is active only while its associated Stance is active.

## Orders: 1

You possess **Order Dice**, which represent your tactical advantage in combat. Your Order die increases as shown in the Sovereign Progression table.

You can hold a maximum of 5 Order Dice. When you finish a Rest, you lose any unused Order Dice.

When a creature within **10m** of you Strikes a target, you spend one Order Die and add it to the Strike's damage.

## Tactical Maneuvers: 1

As an **Action**, issue one of the following Tactical Maneuvers to a creature within **20m** that can see or hear you. The maneuver remains active until the start of your next turn. The first time its Trigger occurs, you gain one Order Die.

### Reposition

The target gains the benefits of the **Disengage Action**.

**Trigger:** The target moves at least 3m.

### Opportunistic Positioning

The target gains one additional **Reaction** until the start of your next turn.

**Trigger:** The target uses a Reaction.

### Brace

The target gains a bonus to Saves equal to your Mental Modifier. This bonus applies to Saves associated with the Ability you chose as your Physical Modifier and the Ability you chose as your Mental Modifier.

**Trigger:** The target succeeds on a Save.

### Hold the Line

The target gains the benefits of the **Dodge Action**.

**Trigger:** A Strike against the target misses, or the target succeeds on an Agility Save.

## Advanced Maneuvers: 2

As an **Action**, choose a creature within **20m** that can see or hear you and spend one Order. The target regains Stamina equal to one roll of your Order Die and gains one of the following benefits.

### Directed Strike

Once during its next turn, the target may make one Strike without spending an Action.

### Martial Prowess

Until the end of its next turn, the target gains the benefits of your active Stance.

### Group Up

The target may immediately move up to half its Speed. When it starts its next turn, choose one ally within 2m of it. That ally regains Stamina equal to the amount the target regained from this Advanced Maneuver.

### Helpful Words

The target has **Weal** on its next Threshold Test.

## Versatile Tactics: 5

Choose one of the following benefits. You may change your choice when you finish a Minor Rest or a Rest.

**Tactical Maneuvers.** When you use Tactical Maneuvers, you may spend one Order Die to target one additional creature with the same maneuver.

**Advanced Maneuvers.** When you use Advanced Maneuvers, you may spend one Order Die to target one additional creature with the same maneuver.

## Recuperate: 6

When you are the Director or Cook, you add your Order Die to the result of your Recreation Test.

## Warlord’s Expertise: 9

Choose one skill in which you have Training. You gain Expertise in that skill.

## Advanced Leadership: 10

As two Actions, you can apply an Advanced Maneuver to creatures of your choosing within a **3m radius** of a point you can see within **20m**. This feature expends only one Order Die, regardless of how many creatures are affected.

Each target receives the Stamina recovery and benefit of the chosen Advanced Maneuver.

## Unbreakable Will: 11

You have **Weal** on Saves made to resist or end the **Charmed** or **Frightened** conditions.

## Sovereign Intuition: 13

Choose a Minor Perk with the **Social** tag. You gain that Perk without spending one of your Perk choices.

## Warlord’s Expertise II: 15

Choose one skill in which you have Training. You gain Expertise in that skill.

## Tireless Leader: 17

When you enter combat, you gain two Orders.

## Improved Orders: 18

When a creature benefits from **Hold the Line** or **Reposition**, its Speed increases by 3m while that Tactical Maneuver is active.

Additionally, when a Sovereign feature restores Stamina to a creature, that creature also regains Stamina equal to your Mental Modifier.

## Advanced Leadership II: 20

Advanced Leadership now costs **one Action**, and its radius increases to **6m**.
