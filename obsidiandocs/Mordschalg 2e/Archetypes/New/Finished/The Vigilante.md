## Physical and Mental Modifiers

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Vigilante features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health

A Vigilante begins with 3 Vitality, 1 Flesh, 8 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training

As a Vigilante, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Skills:** Choose any two skills.

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stance: 1

At level one, you gain access to a Stance of your choosing. While active, you have all features of that Stance. At later levels you will gain new Stances which you may swap between on a Minor Rest. Losing the old features and gaining the new until you choose to swap back to that Stance.

A perk with a Stance prerequisite is active only while its associated Stance is active.

## Combo: 1

A Vigilante can chain rapid strikes together, maintaining momentum until their rhythm falters.

As an Action, Strike a creature and initiate a Combo by declaring that you are going to roll any number of d6 dice between 1 and 4. The Strike deals damage equal to the dice rolled + your Physical Modifier. 

If any of the dice show a 1 the Strike is treated as if it rolled Miss, regardless of the enemy's PD.

## Impeccable Defense: 1

You are adept at avoiding attacks through discipline, agility, and instinct.

While you are not wearing armor, your PD = your Physical Modifier + your Mental Modifier. Also while not wearing armor, your speed is increased by 2m.

## Offensive Posture: 2

You can commit to a relentless offensive rhythm, sacrificing flexibility for sustained pressure.

When you use an Action to initiate a Combo, you may enter your Offensive Posture until the start of your next turn. While in this stance, you have Weal on your Strikes.

If a Strike in your Combo results in a Miss after applying Weal and Woe, the Combo ends.

## Redirection: 5

Your fast reflexes and honed physical capabilities allow you to snatch your enemies attacks from the air, and redirect them towards an unfortunate soul of your choosing.

When a Strike deals physical damage to you, you may use your Reaction to reduce the damage roll by 3d6.
If this reduces the damage to 0 HP, you may spend **2 Strain** to redirect the attack. Choose a creature you can see within 1m if the Strike was melee, or within 12m if it was ranged.
You then Strike that creature. The damage roll for this strike is always 3d6 damage, where the damage type is that of the redirected attack.

## Acrobatic Movement: 6

You move through hazardous terrain with extraordinary control.

When you take the Dash Action, you can move along walls and across calm liquid surfaces as though they were ordinary terrain for the rest of your turn. If you end your turn on a wall or liquid surface, you fall.

While moving, you ignore the negative effects of burning surfaces, sharp surfaces such as broken glass, caltrops, slick surfaces, acidic surfaces, and thin terrain such as tightropes.
This protection does not apply if you end your turn on one of these surfaces.

## Focused Combo: 9

The blows you can connect as part of your Combo are truly staggering.

You roll d8 dice instead of d6 when rolling damage for Combo and Redirection.

## Dazzling  Evasion: 10

Your movements leave opponents struggling to predict where you will be next.

When you are subjected to an AGI or MIT Save and that Save is part of an area, you can use your Reaction to move up to your speed before the effect takes place, possibly out of the area.

## Defensive Posture: 11

Whenever you lull into a more defensive position, you leave yourself better equipped to analyze your opponent.

When you take the Dodge Action, you gain one additional Reaction until the start of your next turn.

## Flashy Movement: 14

When you would take damage from a fall, you can use your Reaction to reduce the resulting damage by one degree, to a minimum of Miss.

Additionally your speed increases by 1m.

## Fluid Rhythm: 15

You learn to recover quickly when a Combo falters.

Once per turn, when you roll a 1 on your Combo Die, you may reroll it and use the new result.

## Deflect Energy: 17

Your ability to intercept attacks extends beyond ordinary physical force.

You can now use Redirection against Strikes that deal any damage type, not just physical damage. When you do so, your Strike's damage type is that of the deflected attack.

## Perseverance: 18

Your discipline allows you to overcome conditions that would hinder others.

At the end of your turn, you may end one of the following conditions on yourself: **Charmed**, **Poisoned**, or **Frightened**.

## Slippery Mind and Feet: 20

Your evasive instincts extend beyond physical reflexes.

You can use Evasion against any Save, regardless of which ability is used for that Save. 

Additionally when a Creature rolls Miss or Minor on a Strike against you and it is their turn, they must use their next Action to Strike again, and that Strike is always a Miss. If they do not have another Action, they cannot make the Strike.
