Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen** or **Instinct** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Spellblade features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Training

As a Spellblade, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light armor, Medium Armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, Martial Weapons
**Skills:** Choose two from Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, History, Insight, Investigation, and Perception

## Spellcasting: 2

At 2nd level, you learn three Spell Formulas. You learn additional formulas as specified by your class boons.

You cast spells by combining a Formula with a Delivery. The Formula determines the spell's effect, while the Delivery determines its targets or area. Casting takes at least 1 Action, and for each Action you devote to casting, you may invest up to 2 Strain. Each Strain spent on damage adds 1d4 damage of the spell's type, while a spell's effect requires 2 Strain to activate. You may also spend Strain to modify a spell's Delivery. The spell takes effect when you finish casting.

A spell normally lasts until the start of your next turn. You may spend 1 Strain at that point to maintain Focus, extending the spell for one additional turn. You can continue spending 1 Strain at the start of each subsequent turn to maintain Focus, or end it at any time. Taking Major damage or Moderate Mental damage breaks your Focus, immediately ending the spell.

## Arcane Reflexes: 6

When a creature within 60 feet of you casts a spell, you can use your **reaction** to gain one **Arcanum Charge**.

Additionally, you are immune to the negative effects of all of your spells

## Charge Capacity Increase: 9

Your capacity for **Arcanum Charges** increases. The maximum number of charges you can hold now equals your **Mental Modifier** + your **Proficiency Bonus**.

## Potent Cantrip Strike: 10

Whenever you hit a creature with a weapon and don't make a cantrip strike, the target takes an extra 1d6 **force** damage.

## Weave Strike: 11

When you hit a creature with an attack roll you can spend 1 **Arcanum Charge** and expend a **Secondary Spell Slot** to make one weapon attack and cast a spell using that slot simultaneously. The spell manifests through the strike, using its normal range and effects.

The spell's target or area of effect originates on the creature you hit with the weapon attack. If the spell would normally require an attack roll, that roll is replaced by the success of your weapon attack; if your weapon attack hits, the spell hits. If the spell requires a saving throw, the targets have disadvantage.

## Sharp Mind

You have advantage on concentration saving throws.
