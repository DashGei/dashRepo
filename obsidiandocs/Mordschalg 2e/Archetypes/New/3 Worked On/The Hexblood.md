## Physical and Mental Modifiers

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Champion features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Training

As a Hexblood, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Skills:** Choose two from Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, and Survival

## Perks

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stances

At level 1, you gain access to two Stances of your choice. You can have only one Stance active at a time. As an Action, you can switch to another Stance you know. When you switch, you lose the features of your previous Stance and gain the features of your new Stance. You retain the new Stance's features until you switch again.

A Perk with a Stance prerequisite is active only while its associated Stance is active.

## Aura of Doom: 1

When an Ally within 2m of you is missing any Vitality or Flesh, you add one Hemo Die in Necrotic damage to all Strikes you make. This die is a d4 when you are level 1, and increases in size according to your features table.

## Bloodhound: 1

You have Weal on Perception and Survival checks made to track a creature with any missing Vitality or Flesh. 

## Transfusion: 2

As an Action you can remove 1 Flesh Hit Point from yourself. When you do so, an Ally within your aura gains 2 Stamina.

## Pain Bank: 4

When an ally within your Aura has any missing Vitality or Flesh, you add your Hemo Die in Necrotic damage to Strikes you make.

## Shadowfell Magicks: 6

If you consume a drop of a creature's blood, you forge a temporary supernatural bond. The creature makes a Physical DC PRE Save. On a failure, for 1 hour you know the cardinal direction to that creature, and you know when it is lying to you. After the hour ends or on a save, that creature becomes immune to this effect until you finish a Moderate Rest.

## Comfortably Numb: 7

Allied creatures within your Aura of Doom have resistance to Necrotic and Pain damage.

## Lightheaded: 9

Your speed increases by 1m, and an additional 1m when you are missing any Vitality or Flesh.

## Hand of Doom: 10

When a creature takes Moderate damage from Pact of Doom, they have Woe on their next saving throw within 10 minutes.

## Deepened Pact: 11

The radius of your Aura of Doom increases to 3m.

## Double the Pain: 14

As a free action on your turn you can remove 1 Point of Physical Health, after taking this damage you gain the benefits of Pain Bank until the start of your next turn.

## Double the Pain: 15

When you take Pain damage, you add the amount you took to your next Strike. The extra damage is Necrotic, and you can only have one instance of this damage stored at a time.

You can also choose to drop or regain your Pain resistance at any time in order to benefit more from this feature.
