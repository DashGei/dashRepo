## Training

As a Berserker, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below:

**Armor:** Light armor, Medium armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons

## Rage: 1

You can enter your Rage by spending 2 Strain as an Action. You gain the benefits of rage for the next 10 minutes or until you end it as a Free Action.

**While Raging, you gain the following benefits:**

- **Enraged Damage** You add an additional d4 whenever you make a Strike. This die increases in size as shown by your class features table and the damage type is that of the Strike.
    
- **Critical Hits** You have the **Criticals** Moderate perk.
    
- **Rampaging** The number you need to roll on the d12 in order to score a Critical is reduced by 1 every time you start your turn while raging.
    
- **Physical Resistance.** You have Resistance to Blunt, Pierce, and Slash damage.

**Duration.** Rage lasts for **10 minutes**, or it ends early if you take an action to do so.

**Critical Hit Range Reset.** When you score a Critical Hit, your crit range resets to 12.

## Adrenaline Rush: 5

Your speed increases by 1m, and whenever a creature other than you within **2m** takes an Action which increases their Speed, your Speed increases by **3m** until the start of your next turn.

## Furious: 6

When an ally within **3m** of you takes damage, your Critical range increases by 1.

## Brutal Critical: 9

When you score a Critical Hit while raging, your damage roll is **Limitless** if your choose to spend 1 Strain.

## Adrenaline Rush II: 10

While Raging you have Resistance to Pain damage.

## All In: 11

When you take the Strike action while raging, you can choose one of the following benefits to apply to your melee Strikes this turn:

- **Focused Fury.** You gain **+1d4 bonus** to Strikes.

- **Reckless Abandon.** Rolling a crit adds the d12's result to your Strike.

Using either feature means that **all Strikes against you have Weal** until the start of your next turn.

## Relentless Rage: 14

While raging, you ignore the effects of missing Stamina and Flesh. You still take damage, but you suffer no penalties until you are at **0 Vitality**.

## For the Fences: 15

When rolling with Weal or Woe, you roll one additional critical d12.

## Insurmountable: 17

When you are hit with an attack that also applies an effect (such as fear, poison, or a condition), you can take **3 Strain** to make a DT **4 MIG Save**. On a success, you take the damage but suffer none of the additional effect.

## Unstoppable: 18

You ignore the effects of Difficult Terrain, and when you are grappled your speed is instead decreased by 3m. Moving while grappled drags the creature grappling you.

## Indomitable Might: 20

You count as Gargantuan for the purpose of grappling, being grappled, and carry capacity.
