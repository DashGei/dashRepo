## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Champion features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Base Statistics

A Berserker begins with 3 Vitality, 2 Flesh, 9 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training

As a Berserker, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below:

**Armor:** Light armor, Medium armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons
**Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Intimidation, Brawling, Acrobatics, Stealth, Perception, Survival, Insight.

## Rage: 1

You can enter your Rage by spending 2 Strain as an Action. You gain the benefits of rage for the next 10 minutes or until you end it as a Free Action.

**While Raging, you gain the following benefits:**

- **Critical Hits** You have the **Criticals** Moderate perk.
    
- **Rampaging** The number you need to roll in order to score a Critical is reduced by 1 every time you start your turn while raging.
    
- **Physical Resistance.** You have Resistance to Blunt, Pierce, and Slash damage.

**Duration.** Rage lasts for **10 minutes**, or it ends early if you take an action.

**Critical Hit Range Reset.** When you score a Critical Hit, your crit range resets to 12.

## Unarmored Resilience: 1

While you are wearing any armor, your PD = your MIG + your AGI.

## Incredible Strength: 3

When raging and you roll for an Athletics or Brawn Check, you also roll your bonus d12 critical die. Rolling a 12 elevates your roll to Major, and lets you complete that Check as a feat of incredible strength if you so choose.

This is one such as pushing through a foot of masoned stone, tearing iron bars from their place, or holding a door shut against a hoard.

## Rage Action: 6

When you take the Strike action while raging, you can choose one of the following benefits to apply to your melee Strikes this turn:

- **Focused Fury.** You gain **+1d4 bonus** to Strikes this turn.
    
- **Reckless Abandon.** Rolling a crit adds the d12's result to your Strike.

**Using** either feature means that all Strikes against you have Weal until the start of your next turn.

## Brutal Critical: 8

When you score a Critical Hit while raging, your damage roll is **Limitless**.


### Insurmountable: 9

When you are hit with an attack that also applies an effect (such as fear, poison, or a condition), you can take **1 Stamina damage** to make a **MIG check**. The DT equals 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher. On a success, you take the damage but suffer none of the additional effect.



### Relentless Rage: 11

While raging, you ignore the effects of missing Stamina and Flesh. You still take damage, but you suffer no penalties until you are at **0 Vitality**.

### For the Fences

When rolling with Weal or Woe, you roll one additional critical d12.
