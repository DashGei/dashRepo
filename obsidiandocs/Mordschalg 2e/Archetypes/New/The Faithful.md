To be a Warlock is to draw hushed whispers from passersby. Your very essence is likely an affront to at least one god's teachings, and the actions of those who precede you can be devastating. So keep your head, if not for your own sake, then for those who look upon you.

Unlike other spellcasters a Warlock's relationship with their powers is tenuous. Some make bargains with incredible beings and some are unwilling, their powers and charge bestowed upon them by blood or speculative proclamations that they are indeed the chosen.

## Spellcasting: 0

At 1st level, you learn three Spell Formulas and one Ritual of your choice. You learn additional formulas and rituals as specified by your class boons.

You cast spells by combining a Formula with a Delivery. The Formula determines the spell's effect, while the Delivery determines its targets or area. Casting takes at least 1 Action, and for each Action you devote to casting, you may invest up to 2 Strain. Each Strain spent on damage adds 1d6 damage of the spell's type, while a spell's effect requires 1 Strain to activate. You may also spend Strain to modify a spell's Delivery. The spell takes effect when you finish casting.

A spell normally lasts until the start of your next turn. You may spend 1 Strain at that point to maintain Focus, extending the spell for one additional turn. You can continue spending 1 Strain at the start of each subsequent turn to maintain Focus, or end it at any time. Taking Major damage or Moderate Mental damage breaks your Focus, immediately ending the spell.

### Rituals

Rituals are distinct magical workings, each with its own Casting Time, Components, and effects. Unlike spells, rituals need not follow the standard rules for Formula and Delivery. Follow each ritual's description to perform it and resolve its effects. Ritual components are consumed when the ritual specifies.

## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Some abilities and features refer to your **Physical Modifier** or **Mental Modifier**. These are not separate ability scores. Instead, each represents one of your existing Ability Modifiers, allowing you to use your strongest relevant attribute.

Your Physical Modifier is based on either Might or Agility. Your Mental Modifier is based on Acumen, Instinct, or Presence. Your class determines which abilities you may choose from.

Whenever a rule refers to one of these modifiers, use the modifier of the ability you selected. For example, your Mental Defense (MD) is equal to your Mental Modifier.

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Divine Declaration

At 1st level

As an Action you can make a Divine Declaration. Choose up to 3 creatures you can see within 12m and at once declare all of them allies of your god, or enemies of your god.

You can only have one declaration active at a time, and they remain as declared for the next 10 minutes.

## Divine Intervention: 10

Across your adventures and exploits, you have truly made a name for yourself among divinity, and although the divine rarely interact with the mortal realm

As two Actions you present your holy symbol. Spend any amount of Favor with one god. Roll a number of d8 dice equal to the amount of Favor you spent. Creatures within 12m of you are either healed equal to the roll, or take damage equal to the roll (damage type is any from a spell you know). 

Once you use this feature, you cannot do so until you finish a Major Rest. 


## Light in the Black: Level 20

You can use this feature to revive all creatures within 15m who died within the last year. All creatures of your choice within range also gain the benefits of a Major Rest. This power is incredibly destructive though, and using it martyrs you instantly. 

When killed in this way your body is destroyed, and your soul instantly ascends to the outer planes, where you become an angel of your god. The effects of this are irreversible.
