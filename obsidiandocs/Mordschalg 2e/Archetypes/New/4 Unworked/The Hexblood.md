## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

Choose **Might** or **Agility** for your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct**, or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Champion features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health: 0

A Hexblood begins with 1 Vitality, 4 Flesh, 9 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training

As a Hexblood, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Skills:** Choose two from Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, and Survival

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stances: 1

At level 1, you gain access to two Stances of your choice. You can have only one Stance active at a time. As an Action, you can switch to another Stance you know. When you switch, you lose the features of your previous Stance and gain the features of your new Stance. You retain the new Stance's features until you switch again.

A Perk with a Stance prerequisite is active only while its associated Stance is active.

## Aura of Doom: 1

You emanate a 10 foot sphere of malice. Once per turn when a creature takes damage within this aura you can cause that creature to take Necrotic damage equal to one instance of Hemomance (listed in class features), and another creature of your choice within the emantation regains hitpoints equal to the instance.

## Bloodhound: 1

You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks made to track a creature that is below its hit point maximum.

## Martial Feats: 2

You have trained in special techniques that give you an edge in combat and beyond. You gain access to martial feats, as described in the **Martial Feats** section. You may only use one **Feat Slot** per turn.

**Feat Slots.** You have 2 **Feat Slots**, which you expend to activate your feats. You regain all expended slots when you finish a short rest. Your number of **Feat Slots** increases as you gain levels, as shown in the Feats Progression table.

**Feats Prepared.** You prepare 2 martial feats of your choice from your feat list. The Feats Prepared column of the Feats Progression table shows when you learn more feats. Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can replace one feat you have prepared with another feat of your choice from your class feat list.

**Feat Tier.** You can learn feats of Tier 1 at level 2. At higher levels you gain access to higher tiers, as shown in the table.

## Pact of Doom: 2

As an **action** you take damage equal to your level (this damage cannot be reduced or prevented in any way). After you take this damage, creatures of your choice (other than you) within your aura regain hitpoints equal to one roll of your Hemo Die, and your next damage roll deals bonus Necrotic damage equal to the amount of hit points restored with this feature.

This feature requires the expendature of a **Feat Slot**.

## Monstrous Adaptation: 3

_You choose a [Monstrous Adaptation](sc_hexblood.html) that defines how you channel your vitality._ Your choice grants you features at 3rd, 7th, 14th, and 20th level.

## Ability Score Improvement

At 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 1. You can't increase an ability score above 5 using this feature.

## Pain Bank: 4

When an ally within your Aura is under half their hitpoint maximum, you add one instance of **Hemomancy** to damage rolls.

## Martial Fatigue Resistance: 5

Taking the **Attack action** does not inflict you with the **fatigued** condition.

## Shadowfell Magicks: 6

If you consume a drop of a creature's blood, you forge a temporary supernatural bond. The creature makes a **Physical DC** Charisma saving throw. On a failure, for 1 hour you know the cardinal direction to that creature, and you know when it is lying to you. After the hour ends, that creature becomes immune to this effect until you finish a long rest.

## Crimson Chain: 6

Once per turn, when an allied creature you can see within 10 feet of you takes damage, you regain 1d4 temporary hitpoints.

## Comfortably Numb: 7

Friendly creatures within your Aura of Doom have resistance to Necrotic and Cold damage.

## Fleet of Foot: 9

Your speed increases by 10 feet, and an additional 5 feet when you are under half your hitpoint maximum.

## Hand of Doom: 10

When a creature takes the Necrotic damage from Pact of Doom, they have Disadvantage on their next saving throw within 10 minutes.

## Deepened Pact: 11

The radius of your Aura of Doom increases to 15 feet.

## Double the Pain: 13

As a free action on your turn you can take damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus (this damage cannot be reduced or prevented in any way), after taking this damage you gain the benefits of Pain Bank until the start of your next turn.

## Font of Vitality: 15

When you enter your Pain Bank, all creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you regain hit points equal to one roll of your **Hemo Die**.

## Die Another Day: 18

You flood a dying ally with necromantic energy, snatching them from death's door. As a **reaction** when an allied creature is reduced to 0 hit points, you can revive them. The target immediately regains 1 hit point, then gains the following benefits:

- **Bleeding Shield.** They gain temporary hit points equal to the total rolled on a number of your **Hemo Dice** equal to your **Mental Modifier** (minimum 1). At the start of each of their turns, they lose temporary hit points equal to one roll of your **Hemo Die**. This loss cannot be reduced or prevented. If this reduces their temporary hit points to 0, the effect ends immediately.
- **Sanguine Strikes.** Once per turn when they hit a creature with a melee attack, they deal extra **necrotic** damage equal to one roll of your **Hemo Die**. They regain hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt. This healing cannot exceed their hit point maximum.

The effect ends early if they are incapacitated. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again.
