## Physical and Mental Modifiers: 0

**Agility** is your Physical Modifier. Then choose **Acumen**, **Instinct** or **Presence** as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Warlock features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

## Health

A Rogue begins with 3 Vitality, 1 Flesh, 8 Stamina, and 9 Mental Health

## Training

As a Rogue, you have Training in a few areas according to the list below: 

**Armor:** Light armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, hand crossbows, light crossbows, scimitars, rapiers, shortswords
**Skills:** Choose four from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth

## Perks: 1

Perks grant talents, techniques, and specialized capabilities that customize your character. Each Perk has a degree which is either Minor, Moderate, or Major, and may have tags or prerequisites that determine who can choose it and how it works.

At 1st level, choose one Minor Perk for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Stance: 1

At level one, you gain access to a Stance of your choosing. While active, you have all features of that Stance. At later levels you will gain new Stances which you may swap between on a Minor Rest. Losing the old features and gaining the new until you choose to swap back to that Stance.

Any perk you gain which requires that Stance is only ever active while that Stance is active.

## Sneak Attack: 1

Once per turn when you Strike with Weal, you can sacrifice that Weal in order to make the Strike Critical, and do extra damage.

Once per turn, you can deal extra damage to one creature you Strike if you have Weal on the Strike. The Strike must be made with Agility. You don't need advantage if another enemy of the target is within 1m of it, that enemy isn't incapacitated, and you don't have Woe on the Strike.

The extra damage starts at 1d6 and increases as shown in the Sneak Attack column of the Rogue table. The damage type is the same as the weapon's type.

## Jack of all Trades: 1

You can add half your **Proficiency Bonus** to any ability check you make that doesn't already include your **Proficiency Bonus**. Additionally, choose two of your skill proficiencies to gain expertise in.

## Roguish Archetype: 3

_You choose a [Roguish Archetype](sc_rogue.html) that emulates your approach to roguery._ Your choice grants you features at 3rd level, and then again at 7th, 14th, and 20th level.

## Quick Feet: 3

When you take the **Dash action**, you gain an additional 15 feet of movement for that turn.

## Ability Score Improvement

At 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 1. You can't increase an ability score above 5 using this feature.

## Uncanny Dodge: 5

When an attacker that you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your **reaction** to halve the attack's damage against you.

## Exploit: 6

When you make an attack as part of a reaction, that attack is always a Sneak Attack and does not count towards your per turn limit of Sneak Attacks.

## Expertise: 8

Choose two of your skill proficiencies to gain expertise in.

## Evasion: 9

When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a **Dexterity saving throw** to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed, and only half damage if you fail.

## Quick Feet II: 10

When you take any **reaction**, you can move up to your speed as part of that reaction.

## Reliable Talent: 11

Whenever you make an ability check that lets you add your **Proficiency Bonus**, if the roll doesn't have advantage or disadvantage, roll two d20s and use whichever roll is closer to 11. If there is a tie (for example, 9 and 13), you take the higher roll.

## Opportunistic Strike: 12

When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you rolls a natural 1 on an attack roll or saving throw, you can use your **reaction** to make an attack against that creature.

## Skill Specialization: 13

Choose one skill or tool proficiency that you are proficient in. Your **Proficiency Bonus** is doubled for any ability check you make that uses that skill or tool. This stacks with Expertise (you can choose a skill you already have Expertise in, but the bonus does not double again; instead you gain advantage on checks with that skill).

Alternatively, you can choose to gain proficiency in two additional skills of your choice.

## Blindsense: 15

If you are able to hear, you are aware of the location of any hidden or invisible creature within 10 feet of you.

## Dirty Tactics: 15

Whenever you roll with **advantage**, you roll three d20s and take the highest result.

## Slippery Mind: 17

Your cunning mind is difficult to assault. You gain proficiency in **Wisdom saving throws**.

## Elusive: 18

No attack roll has **advantage** against you while you aren't **incapacitated**.
