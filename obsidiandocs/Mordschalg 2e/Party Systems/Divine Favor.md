# Divine Favor

**For many in the world of Gaia**, Religion plays a communal and utilitarian role in one's life. Rarely will individuals devote themselves to singular gods, or swear all of them off outright.

A typical peasant may pray to Gri in the morning for a safe crop, evoke Ilxi's name while gambling with their friends, and then go home where an emblem of Helm sits above the door and guards against evils.

### Player Interactions

Just like everyone else, players can form relationships with the various gods of the world, gaining boons, or possibly minor woes if they repeatedly offend some deities.

Not every god prefers the same method of worship. Some gods wish only for larger and larger masses of dedicated worshippers, some simply want their followers to practice their disciplines, and others care solely that their ideals are spread.

### Divine Favor

The party loses or gains **Favor Points** by supporting a deity's efforts and ideals as detailed in each deity's Favor Criteria section, at the DM's discretion. Unmentioned actions that the party takes may affect relationships with the gods.

Favor grants the entire party small, thematic blessings. Acting against a god's principles can bring minor woes. A party who makes meaningful connections with each other and the world will always bring favor.

When the party finishes a long rest together, they may ask the DM to reveal their current standings with the gods. They may also choose to voluntarily decrease their Favor with any deity by any amount at that time to make room for other relationships.

### Favor Scale

| Favor Score | Status     | Effect        |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------- |
| -5          | Disfavored | Moderate Woe  |
| -3          | Displeased | Minor Woe     |
| 0           | Neutral    | -             |
| 1 - 2       | Noticed    | -             |
| 4           | Favored    | Minor Boon    |
| 7           | Honored    | Moderate Boon |
| 15          | Champion   | Major Boon    |

Favor Points adjust at the end of a long rest or session at the DM's discretion. The party cannot lose or gain more than 5 FP per session with a given god through direct offense, and minor slights may not change the score at all.

The party can hold a maximum of **30 Favor Points** invested across a maximum of 5 deities at any time. Negative Favor Points do not add to this total, and the party can have a maximum of 10 total negative Favor Points invested. If the party would gain FP that would push their total above 30, those points are lost.

### Worship Methods

Every deity has a **Worship Method** that dictates how the party gains Favor. The DM awards 1 FP when the party meaningfully fulfills the method, and occasionally 2 FP for exceptional acts.

|Method|Description|
|---|---|
|**Devotion**|The god grows stronger through worship. FP is earned by spreading the faith, building shrines, converting others, or leading communal prayers.|
|**Discipline**|The god values the practice of a craft, skill, or way of life. FP is earned by engaging in the associated discipline. Creating art, mastering a trade, or upholding a code may be the best way to earn this god's favor.|
|**Ideals**|The god cares that their ideals are enacted. FP is earned by championing those ideals, defeating opposing forces, or shifting a community's values.|

### Boons and Woes

Boons are occasional minor bonuses that the party may obtain by maintaining good relationships with gods. Each god has their own set of boons. Some may give players resources to spend during the day, while others may only activate when the DM chooses.

Some Boons and Woes require tracking the Velthraen Calendar. Additionally, if a Boon or Woe affects one party member once per day or week, it only occurs the first time that party member triggers it, ignoring subsequent triggers by other party members until the Boon or Woe refreshes.
