## 2: Stances

Gives you a passive, a reaction, and a once per turn action. A creature can switch between them with their turn start action. A creature knows 2 at level 1, and gains an additional 

Pros: Neatly groups your maneuvers and gives you a simplified list to choose from. Constrictive enough to the point where it requires forward thinking and commitment.

Cons: Some stances may thematically clash with character archetypes who will choose them. Need to be balanced against each other.

To solve some of the cons, it could be fun to allow players to create one stance themselves at level 5 so they have at least one stance they feel is emblematic of their character's fighting style. Characters can unlock one more at levels 10, 15, and 20.


## 3: extensive basic action/reaction system

Gives an extensive list of basic actions/reactions to take, so parrying is less of an ability, and instead is something you can just do.


## 4: Symmetrical to spells

You know a certain amount of basic maneuvers which you can spend strain to modify each maneuver into doing what you want it to do.



# Always available examples:

These maneuvers should always be accessible to anyone proficient with their weapon. Primarily as they don't require any extreme techniques, and they aren't too powerful.

**Parry** {R*} When attacked with a strike dealing physical damage, you can make a melee strike with their weapon as the target. If your strike deals more damage then theirs, their strike becomes a miss.

**Brace** {R*} When a creature you can see moves into your reach, you can use your reaction make one melee weapon strike against that creature.

**Trip** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon strike you can for the target true make a **Physical DC** Strength saving throw. On a failure, it falls prone.

**Sweep Strike** When you hit a creature with a melee strike, you can make another strike with the same weapon against a creature adjacent to the target within reach.

**Sweet Spot** When a creature ends their turn within 30-40 feet from you, you can use your reaction to  make a ranged weapon strike against the creature. When you finish a short rest you can change the range of the sweet spot to any 10 foot range between 10-150 feet.

**Pushing Attack** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon strike, you can push it up to 2 meters directly away from you. This has no effect if the creature is two sizes larger than you.

**Opportunity Attack** When a hostile creature that you can see moves out of your reach, you can use your reaction to make one melee weapon strike against that creature before they leave, reducing their speed by 2 meters if the strike did Moderate damage.

**Chokeout** When you are grappling a creature that is the same size as you or smaller, you can take one action to force the enemy to make a Physical DC Strength saving throw. On a failure they begin suffocating and immediately are considered choking. A creature repeats the saving throw at the start of its turn regaining breath if they succeed, but they do not break the grapple. If you inflict all 10 levels of exhaustion this way you can choose to let the creature fall unconscious for an hour instead of them dying.

While choking a creature you cannot take reactions or actions, and both hands are occupied. If you take damage while choking a creature, the creature gets to make another saving throw.

**Sentinel Strike** When a creature within 5 feet of you makes a strike against a target other than you, you can use your reaction to make a melee weapon strike against the attacking creature.

# Sometimes Available, maybe requires a check to perform/requires a resource/Tradeoff

These abilities might require a resource to be expended, or require a tradeoff/check to be executed, such as Daggerpush also making you prone, or Take it and Leave putting you down a weapon. These are slightly more powerful/extraordinary than most maneuvers, but also look a little weird in a fighting style.

**Ricochet** Use this feat to take the attack action against a creature. This attack roll ignores cover as long as there is one surface to bounce off. A bounced surface takes 1d4 physical damage, and you use the total distance traveled to and between bounced surfaces when determining range.

**Counter Posing** As an action, you can use this feat to enter an immovable stance that lasts until you move from your space. For the duration, when a creature attempts to Grapple you, move you, or move through you, it first must make a Physical DC Strength saving throw. On a failure, you Grapple it or knock it Prone.

**Daggerpush** With two actions you target a prone creature within 5 feet of you. You also become prone, and the creature makes a Phys DC Strength check, on a failure you hit the creature with an automatic critical hit using a weapon you are holding.

**Take it and Leave it** When you deal Flesh or Vitality damage to a creature with a Slashing or Piercing melee weapon, you can leave the weapon in the enemy. While the weapon is lodged, the target subtracts 1d4 from all rolls they make. A creature can remove the weapon with an action, taking 1d4 Mental damage when they remove it. 

The amount of dice is increased for every size smaller the creature is than huge, and every weight the weapon is above minor.

**Blinding Strike** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can activate this feat. The target must make a **Physical DC** Strength saving throw. On a failure, it is blinded until the end of your next turn.
