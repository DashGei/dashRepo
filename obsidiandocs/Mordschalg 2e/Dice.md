## Dice

Mordschlag uses the following polyhedral dice:

- d2
- d4
- d6
- d8
- d10
- d12
- d20

Whenever the rules instruct you to roll a die, the notation indicates both the number of dice and the type of die rolled.

**Examples:**

- 1d6 means one six-sided die.
- 2d8 means two eight-sided dice.
- 3d4 means three four-sided dice.

If a rule tells you to add a modifier, add that value to the result after rolling.

**Example:**

A weapon deals 1d8 + 2 damage. If the d8 shows a 5, the total damage is 7.
If a rule tells you to multiply by a modifier, you roll a number of dice equal to the modifier.

**Example:**

A feature dictates you roll Proficiency Bonus x 1d2. You roll a number of dice equal to your proficiency bonus. It may also be written as PB x 1d2 in shorthand.

### Die Mechanics

Sometimes when you roll dice in Mordschlag, you will be asked to modify the roll using  one of the below mechanics:

### Exploding 

If the number on a die is the highest possible number, you can roll an additional die of that size and add it to the total. Some features may allow the additional die to also explode.

### Maximized

The dice are all considered to have rolled their maximum number. So rolling 2d6 and 1d4 will result in a total of 16. This does not count for exploding dice.

### Set

All rolls under the Modifier are set instead to that Modifier

### Re-Rolled

All rolls equal or under the modifier are re-rolled once.
