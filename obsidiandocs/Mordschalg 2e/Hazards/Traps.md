- Spike Trap. On a failed save, the creature takes piercing damage equal to a number of d6 equal to your proficiency bonus and its speed is halved until the end of its next turn.

- Blinding Trap. On a failed save, the creature is blinded until the end of its next turn.

- Poison Trap. On a failed save, the creature takes poison damage equal to a number of d4 equal to your proficiency bonus and is poisoned for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, it can repeat the saving throw to end the poison.

- Restraining Trap. On a failed save, the creature is Restrained until it uses an action to make a successful Physical DT MIG or DEX Save (its choice) to escape.

- Tripwire. On a failed save, the creature is knocked prone.

- Alarm Trap. A mental alert is sent to you if you are within 1 mile of the trap when it triggers. This trap deals no damage and does not otherwise impede the creature.

- Break Trap. The trap does something else, such as a breaking an oil flask, setting off an explosive, or triggering another item which you expend when you set the trap.
