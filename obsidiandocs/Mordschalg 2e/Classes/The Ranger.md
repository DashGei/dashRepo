## Physical and Mental Modifiers

Some of your abilities will require your **Mental Modifier** or **Physical Modifier**.

Your **Physical Modifier** is your Might or Dexterity modifier.

Your **Mental Modifier** is your Instinct or Intelligence modifier.

## Hit Points

As a Ranger you start with 3 Vitality, 1 Flesh, 10 Stamina, and 9 Psyche

Your Physical Defense (PD) is 6, and your Mental Defense (MD) is 7.

## Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields

**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons

**Skills:** Choose three from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, and Survival

## Hunter's Mark: 1

As an action, designate an enemy you can see within 30 meters as your quarry. While marked, you have advantage on Instinct (Perception or Survival) checks to find or track it. When you hit the marked creature with an attack you add additional dice in Force (P) damage. The dice added are determined by your level as shown in the features table.

When you reduce a marked creature to 0 Vitality with an attack, you may immediately apply **Hunter's Mark** to a new creature without using an action.

The mark lasts until you finish a Moderate rest, the creature dies, you move the mark, or you end it as an action. The creature has no way of knowing it’s marked.

## Analyzed: 3

When you deal damage to a marked creature, you know if they have any resistances, immunities, or vulnerabilities.

Additionally, when you roll for Hunter's Mark damage, you can choose to deal Stress damage instead of Force. When you do so, you also add your Mental Modifier to your Hunter's Mark damage.

## Mortal Reminder: 5

When striking a Distressed creature, or when your **Hunter's Mark** has persisted on the same creature for a minute, you deal maximum damage with your Hunter's Mark dice instead of rolling.

## Hit List: 6

All creatures within 2 meters of your marked target are also marked.

Additionally, when you strike a creature you can move your mark to that creature, no action required.

## Killer Instinct: 8

When you strike, you can add 1 Vitality damage. You can only use this feature if the extra damage would be enough to reduce the creature to 0 Vitality.

## Mystic Mark

When you deal Force damage with Hunter's Mark, you can choose to make the damage Spiritual Force damage instead of Physical. When you do so, you also add your Mental Modifier to your Hunter's Mark damage.

## Analyzed II: 13

When strike a marked creature, instead of dealing extra damage from your **Hunter's Mark** feature, you can instead choose to remove one damage resistance it has until the start of your next turn.

## Lord of the Land: 15

All perks with the **Exploration** tag require 2 less Strain when used.

Additionally, you can use a Strain and 10 minutes to mark a 30 meter radius location with your Hunter's mark feature. While in this radius, you have Weal on Stealth and Perception checks, and all creatures of your choice are marked by you while in this area, and the area disappears if you end the mark as a free action.

## Feral Senses: 18

You have Blindsight, you see invisible creatures and objects as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane out to 6 meters.
