## Physical and Mental Modifiers

Some of your abilities will require your **Mental Modifier** or **Physical Modifier**.

Your **Physical Modifier** is your Strength or Dexterity modifier.

Your **Mental Modifier** is your Wisdom modifier.

## Hit Points

As a Ranger you start with 5 Vitality, 10 Stamina, and 5 Psyche

Your Physical Defense (PD) is 6, and your Mental Defense (MD) is 7.

## Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields

**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons

**Tools:** None

**Saving Throws:** Strength, Dexterity

**Skills:** Choose three from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, and Survival

## Hunter's Mark: 1

As an action, designate an enemy you can see within 90 feet as your quarry. While marked, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception or Survival) checks to find or track it. When you hit the marked creature with an attack you add additional dice in force damage. The dice added are determined by your level as shown in the features table.

When you reduce a marked creature to 0 hit points with an attack, you may immediately apply **Hunter's Mark** to a new creature without using an action.

The mark lasts until you finish a long rest, the creature dies, or you expend a **Feat Slot** as a free action to end it. The creature has no way of knowing it’s marked.

## Aspect Bond: 2

**Aspect Tiers.** Aspects are organized into three tiers of power. You gain access to higher tiers at levels 2, 8, and 14, as shown in the Aspect Tier column.

**Preparing Aspects.** You can have 2 aspects active simultaneously. During a short or long rest, you can meditate and commune with nature to swap out any or all of your active aspects for other aspects in the aspect table.

## Ranger Conclave: 3

_You choose a [Ranger Conclave](https://dashgei.github.io/dashRepo/sc_ranger.html) that embodies your hunting philosophy._ Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and then again at 7th, 14th, and 20th level.

## Analyzed: 3

When you deal damage to a marked creature, you know if they have any resistances, immunities, or vulnerabilities.

## Martial Fatigue Resistance: 5

Taking the **Attack action** does not inflict you with the **fatigued** condition.

## Mortal Reminder: 5

When your **Hunter's Mark** has persisted on the same creature for a minute, you deal maximum damage with your Hunter's Mark dice instead of rolling.

## Hit List: 6

All creatures within 10 feet of your marked target are also marked.

Additionally, when you hit a creature with an attack you can mark that creature, no action or feat slot required.

## Killer Instinct: 8

When you damage a creature with an attack, you can add extra damage equal to twice your **Proficiency Bonus**. You can only use this feature if the extra damage would be enough to reduce the creature to 0 hit points.

## Expanded Aspects: 10

You can have a total of 3 aspects active at one time.

## Analyzed II: 13

When you hit a marked creature with an attack, instead of dealing extra damage from your **Hunter's Mark** feature, you can instead choose to remove one damage resistance it has until the start of your next turn.

Additionally, once per turn when you miss a creature with an attack roll you can immediately make another with disadvantage.

## Lord of the Land: 15

You have all feats with the **Exploration** tag prepared and none of them count against your feats prepared.

Additionally, you can use a Feat Slot and 10 minutes to mark a 150 foot radius location with your Hunter's mark feature. While in this radius, you have advantage on Stealth, Athletics, and Perception checks, and all creatures of your choice are marked by you while in this area, and the area dissapears if you end the mark as a free action.

## Evasion: 17

When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if you fail.

## Feral Senses: 18

You have Blindsight, you see invisible creatures and objects as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane out to 30 feet.
