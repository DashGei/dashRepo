To be a Warlock is to draw hushed whispers from passersby. Your very essence is likely an affront to at least one god's teachings, and the actions of those who precede you can be devastating. So keep your head, if not for your own sake, then those who look upon you.

Unlike other spellcasters a Warlock's relationship with their powers is tenuous. Some make bargains with incredible beings and some are unwilling, their powers and charge bestowed upon them by blood or speculative proclamations that they are indeed the chosen.

### Physical and Mental Modifiers

Agility to determine your Physical Modifier. Then use Acumen as your Mental Modifier. Use the corresponding Ability Modifier whenever one of your Wizard features refers to your Physical Modifier or Mental Modifier.

### Spellcasting

At 1st level, you learn four Spell Formulas and one Ritual of your choice. You learn additional formulas and rituals as specified by your class features.

You cast spells by combining a Formula with a Delivery, creating a magical effect by spending Strain. A spell with a damage base deals 1d6 damage, increasing by 1d6 for every 2 Strain spent on damage. For each Action you devote to casting, you may invest up to 2 Strain. You may spend Strain on damage, Delivery, or other effects as their rules allow. The spell takes effect when you finish casting.

Spells typically last until the start of your next turn. You can maintain Focus using your Turn Start Action to extend a spell's duration by one additional turn. Taking Major Physical damage or Moderate Mental damage breaks your Focus and causes the spell to drop instantly. You can also drop Focus yourself as a Free Action

### Rituals

Rituals are distinct magical workings, each with its own Casting Time, Components, and effects. Unlike spells, rituals need not follow the standard rules for Formula and Delivery. Follow each ritual's description to perform it and resolve its effects. Ritual components are consumed when the ritual specifies.
