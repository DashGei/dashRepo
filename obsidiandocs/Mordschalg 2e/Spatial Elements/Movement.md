### Hidden Movement

Whenever you move, you can use up an equal amount of Hidden Movement to make your movement entirely silent

### Sneak

{AA} 

A creature taking the Sneak action makes a DT 6 Stealth Check, and then gains an amount of Hidden Movement based on the result.

The creature has that Hidden Movement for a number of turns equal to the amount of movement gained. Taking this action replaces the old roll with the new, and does not add new Hidden Movement onto the old Hidden Movement

| Miss | Minor | Moderate | Major |                 |
| ---- | ----- | -------- | ----- | --------------- |
| 0    | 1     | 5        | 10    | Speed/Turns     |
| -    | 5     | 2        | 0     | Notice Distance |


### Search

{AA}

A creature taking the Search action makes a DT 6 Perception Check, and can see any target Hidden at the same degree as your Perception roll.

A creature proficient in Perception always has the benefits of at least a Minor Search Action, even when not taking the Search Action.
