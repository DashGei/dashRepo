### Proficiencies

- **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
    
- **Weapons:** All simple and martial weapons
    
- **Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Intimidation, Wrestling, Acrobatics, Stealth, Perception, Survival, Insight
    

---

### Physical and Mental Modifiers

Some of your abilities will require your **Physical Modifier** or **Mental Modifier**.

- Your **Physical Modifier** is your **Might (MIG)** modifier.
    
- Your **Mental Modifier** is your **Intelligence (INT)**, **Wisdom (WIS)**, or **Presence (PRE)** modifier, as specified.
    

---

### Rage: 1

You can enter your Rage as your **Turn Start Action** by taking **Minor Psyche damage**, provided you aren't wearing heavy armor.

**While Raging, you gain the following benefits:**

- **Crit Die.** Whenever you make a Strike, you roll a separate **1d12**. This die is not added to your damage. If it rolls a **12**, you score a **Critical Hit**.
    
- **Critical Hit Effect.** When you score a Critical Hit, your damage category increases by one step (Minor→Major, Major→Severe, Severe→Severe +1 damage).
    
- **Building Fury.** At the start of each of your turns while raging, and each time an allied creature you can see within 10 feet of you takes damage, your Critical Hit range increases by 1 (from 12 to 11, then 10, etc.), to a minimum of **10**.
    
- **Physical Resistance.** You take half damage (rounded down) from Physical sources (weapons, falls, crushing, etc.). This applies after Armor reduction.


**Duration.** Rage lasts for **10 minutes**. It ends early if you are incapacitated (0 Vitality) or you choose to end it as a free action.

**Critical Hit Range Reset.** When you score a Critical Hit, your crit range resets to 12.

---

### Unarmored Resilience: 1

While you are not wearing any armor and not using a shield, you gain the following benefits:

- **Pain Transfer.** When you take damage, you may choose to apply any amount of that damage to your **Psyche** instead of your Stamina, Flesh, or Vitality, as long as you have **3 or more Psyche** remaining.
    
- **PD Bonus.** Your Physical Defense (PD) increases by **+MIG** (instead of using DEX alone).


Wearing a shield negates these benefits.

---

### Danger Sense: 2

You gain **+1d4 bonus** to all WIS checks to detect traps, ambushes, or hidden enemies. You cannot be blinded, deafened, or incapacitated to gain this benefit.

---

### Primal Path: 3

You choose a Primal Path that shapes the nature of your rage. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level, and then again at 7th, 10th, and 11th level.

---

### Primal Knowledge: 3

You gain proficiency in another skill of your choice from the Berserker skill list. While your Rage is active, you can make ability checks using the following skills as **Might (MIG)** checks: Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival. This represents primal power enhancing your agility, bearing, and senses.

---

### Attribute Increase

At 4th, 7th, and 10th level, you can increase one attribute of your choice by +1. You cannot increase an attribute above 3 using this feature.

---

### Fast Movement: 5

Your movement speed increases by **+1 zone** while you aren't wearing heavy armor.

---

### Rage Action: 6

When you take the Strike action while raging, you can choose one of the following benefits to apply to your melee Strikes this turn:

- **Focused Fury.** You gain **+1d4 bonus** to all damage rolls this turn, but attacks against you gain **+1d4 bonus** until the start of your next turn.
    
- **Reckless Abandon.** When you hit with a melee Strike, you can add **+1 damage** to the result, but attacks against you gain **+1d4 bonus** until the start of your next turn.
    

---

### Brutal Critical: 8

When you score a Critical Hit while raging, your damage increases by an additional **+1** (on top of the normal Critical Hit effect).

---

### Insurmountable: 9

When you are hit with an attack that also applies an effect (such as fear, poison, or a condition), you can take **1 Stamina damage** to make a **MIG check**. The DT equals 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher. On a success, you take the damage but suffer none of the additional effect.

---

### Relentless Rage: 11

While raging, you ignore the effects of missing Stamina and Flesh. You still take damage, but you suffer no penalties until you are at **0 Vitality**.
