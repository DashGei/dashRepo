# Journey Through The Sym: From Silver to Chixie

## Overview of the Route

The journey from Silver (1) to Chixie (6) spans roughly 180 miles through increasingly arid terrain, taking approximately 6-8 days at normal pace, depending on routes chosen and complications encountered. The party will travel with Seymour's smuggling caravan, which follows a winding path through the central hill country to avoid Sym patrols.

---

## The Three Paths

### The Copper Road (Main Trade Route)
*Fastest route, most heavily patrolled*

The Copper Road is the primary artery connecting Silver to the mining operations around Chixie. Well-maintained gravel roads wind through the hills, with waystations every 20-30 miles. Unfortunately, the Sym military uses this road extensively, with patrols passing every 4-6 hours during daylight. Travel at night is possible but dangerous due to bandits and nocturnal predators.

**Travel Time:** 6 days
**Encounter Rate:** High (Sym patrols, military convoys, toll checkpoints)
**Difficulty:** Moderate

### The Winding Way (Smuggler's Path)
*Seymour's preferred route, balanced risk*

A network of game trails, dry creek beds, and abandoned mining tracks that cuts through the central hills. The path requires knowledgeable guides and careful navigation but avoids most Sym presence. Water sources are scarce but known to smugglers. The route passes near several small settlements that are sympathetic to Fair Time's cause.

**Travel Time:** 8 days
**Encounter Rate:** Moderate (bandits, wildlife, environmental hazards)
**Difficulty:** Hard navigation, moderate combat

### The Deep Desert Route
*Slowest but safest from Sym patrols*

Skirting the southern edge of the region through true desert terrain, this route avoids the central hills entirely. It requires carrying significant water supplies and navigating by stars. Only recommended for parties with survival expertise or a local guide. Passes through Tabaxi territory where the party might find allies.

**Travel Time:** 10-12 days
**Encounter Rate:** Low-moderate (desert creatures, environmental challenges)
**Difficulty:** Very hard survival, low combat

---

## Timeline Pressure

The party has **7 days** before Fair Time is scheduled to be transported to the capital. Missing this window means attempting a rescue from a heavily guarded military convoy—not impossible, but significantly more difficult.

**Pace Requirements:**
- Copper Road: Requires pushing hard (forced marches, 50% chance of exhaustion)
- Winding Way: Comfortable pace with room for 1-2 days of delays
- Deep Desert: Only viable if the party has access to fast travel or accepts they'll miss the initial window

## Detailed Encounter Locations (Winding Way)

### Day 1: Leaving Silver
**Distance:** 12 miles
**Terrain:** Transitional scrubland, increasingly rocky

#### Encounter: The Toll of the Road
*Social/Combat Encounter, CR 4*

Two hours outside Silver, the party encounters a Sym checkpoint. A young High Elf lieutenant (use **Veteran** stats with High Elf traits) and four guards (**Guard** stats) are stopping all southbound traffic. They demand to see "travel papers" and are suspicious of anyone heading toward Chixie.

**Options:**
- Pay the bribe (50-100 gp depending on negotiation)
- Let Seymour talk (Deception DC 13, with advantage if party stays quiet)
- Create a distraction (the lieutenant has a gambling problem—mention of cards makes him less vigilant)
- Fight (reinforcements arrive in 1d4+1 rounds if alarm is raised)

**Treasure:** If searched, the checkpoint has a lockbox with 120 gp in "fines collected."

---

### Day 1-2: Camp at the Old Copper Mine
**Distance:** 24 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** Rocky hills, sparse vegetation

*This is the encounter the user provided—expanded for full detail.*

#### The Warning

As evening approaches, the caravan passes a small human settlement—really just six families huddled in a valley. The locals are visibly relieved to see travelers and insist the party share their evening meal. During dinner, an old woman named Marta pulls the party aside.

"You'll be wanting to camp soon. There's only one good spot for miles—the old mine entrance up ahead. But listen well: that place is cursed. The wendigos come out at night. My grandson saw one last winter, all twisted and hungry. Take these."

She presses small iron charms into their hands, each bearing a crude sun symbol.

**The Charms:** Function as a **Holy Symbol** for warding purposes. While carried, undead creatures have disadvantage on Perception checks to detect the bearer. Additionally, once per night, a charm can be activated as an action to create a ward against undead (as **Protection from Evil and Good**, but only against undead, duration 1 hour).

#### The Mine Camp

The mine entrance is indeed the best campsite for miles—flat ground, natural windbreak, and a freshwater spring. The entrance is partially collapsed, with old support timbers visible. Charcoal drawings on the rock face depict stick figures with long claws and wide mouths.

**Setup:** If the party camps here, they can set up basic defenses. Seymour's workers are nervous and will insist on keeping a large fire burning through the night.

#### The Attack

At midnight (or during the third watch), the wendigos emerge.

**Creatures:** 4 **Ghouls** reskinned as wendigos (twisted humanoids with gray, stretched skin, elongated limbs, and frozen blue eyes). One **Ghast** serves as their leader—a former miner who fell to cannibalism during a winter famine.

**Combat Behavior:**
- The wendigos attack from the mine entrance, moving silently (Stealth +6)
- They target isolated party members first
- The Ghast uses its stench ability and coordinates attacks
- They retreat if reduced to 1 creature and will hunt the party another night

**Environmental Effects:**
- The mine entrance provides three-quarters cover for creatures inside
- Old mining equipment can be used as improvised weapons (picks = 1d8 piercing)
- Loose rocks make the area difficult terrain for anyone moving more than half speed

**Treasure in the Mine:** If the party explores (after dealing with the wendigos), they find:
- 35 gp in a rotting leather pouch
- A miner's journal describing the famine and the "hunger that wouldn't end"
- A **+1 Handaxe** buried in a collapsed tunnel (the old foreman's tool)

**Development:** If the party shows the journal to Marta's people on return, they're grateful for the closure and become friendly contacts. They'll provide supplies at half cost and hide the party from Sym patrols in the future.

### Day 3: The Sinkhole
**Distance:** 42 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** Limestone hills, hidden caves

#### Environmental Encounter: The Ground Gives Way

Mid-morning, the lead wagon's wheel breaks through a thin layer of stone, revealing a sinkhole that expands rapidly. The party has moments to react before the road collapses.

**Mechanics:**
- Everyone within 20 feet of the sinkhole makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw
- Failure: fall 30 feet into a limestone cavern (3d6 bludgeoning damage)
- The wagon is half-lost, requiring DC 15 Strength (Athletics) checks to pull free

**The Cavern Below:** The sinkhole reveals an ancient cave system. The walls are covered in faded paintings depicting figures with animal heads and spiral patterns. A successful DC 13 Intelligence (History) check identifies these as pre-Sym civilization art, perhaps 3,500 years old.

**Occupant:** A **Thousand Teeth** has made its nest in the cavern, disturbed by the collapse. It attacks anyone who falls in but can be avoided if the party is quick.

**Discovery:** A skeleton in the corner of the cave wears a necklace with a **Periapt of Wound Closure** (the body is that of an ancient shaman). Taking it requires a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw; on failure, the character is haunted by dreams of the shaman for the next three nights, gaining one level of exhaustion each morning until they return the item or receive a **Remove Curse** spell.

---

### Day 3-4: Vashti's Rest
**Distance:** 58 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** A sheltered valley with a permanent spring

#### The Settlement

Vashti's Rest is a small waystation of about 200 people, mostly human with a significant Tabaxi minority. The town is built around a natural spring that feeds a small orchard—a rarity in this region. The people are neutral toward the Sym but quietly support Fair Time's resistance.

**Notable Locations:**
- **The Dusty Paw:** A tavern run by an elderly Tabaxi woman named Vashti herself (actually the town's founder's daughter, the fifth in a line to use the name)
- **The Grove:** A sacred space maintained by a druid circle (3 **Druids** of low level)
- **Orchard Market:** Local fruits, preserved goods, and water

#### Encounter: The Informant's Dilemma
*Roleplay/Moral Choice*

Vashti approaches the party with a problem. A young human woman named Lira has been accused of selling information to the Sym. Evidence: she was seen talking to a patrol officer, and several resistance members have been arrested since. The town is divided—some want her exiled, others want her killed.

**Investigation:**
- DC 14 Insight: Lira seems genuinely terrified but not guilty
- DC 15 Investigation: Her "conversation" with the patrol was actually about her sick mother's medicine—the officer is her cousin, secretly sympathetic
- DC 13 Perception: A shady character named Bren is watching the proceedings with barely concealed satisfaction

**The Truth:** Bren is the actual informant, framing Lira to divert suspicion. If confronted, he attempts to flee. Capturing him reveals documents proving his guilt and a list of resistance members he's already sold out.

**Resolution Options:**
- Expose Bren: The town is grateful; Vashti provides the party with 2 **Potions of Healing** and information about the road ahead
- Let Lira be exiled: The town becomes less trusting; prices increase 50%
- Kill Bren quietly: Vigilante justice; the town looks the other way but is wary

### Day 5: The Whispering Canyon
**Distance:** 75 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** Narrow canyon, ancient carvings

#### Environmental/Combat Encounter: The Ghosts of the Old Kingdom

This canyon was once a sacred site for the pre-Sym civilization. Thousands of small alcoves are carved into the canyon walls, each holding a funerary urn. The wind through the canyon creates an eerie whistling that sounds like voices.

**Hazard:** The canyon is home to a **Will-o'-Wisp** that has become corrupted over centuries, feeding on the spiritual residue of the dead. It appears as a floating blue light that leads travelers toward a hidden crevasse.

**Creatures:** 
- 1 **Will-o'-Wisp** (uses its consume life ability on those drawn to the crevasse)
- 2d4 **Skeletons** animated from ancient corpses, emerging from the alcoves if the wisp is threatened

**The Crevasse:** A crack in the canyon floor, 40 feet deep. At the bottom lie the bones of those lured here over the years. Among the remains:
- 250 gp in mixed coins
- A **Wand of Magic Detection** (2 charges remaining)
- A sealed clay jar containing ancient wine (worth 150 gp to a collector)

**Avoiding the Encounter:** A DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check allows the party to recognize the danger signs (unusual cold spots, sounds that seem to come from wrong directions) and find a safe path through.

---

### Day 5-6: The Shattered Spire
**Distance:** 88 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** High ridgeline with a ruined tower

#### Landmark

The Shattered Spire is all that remains of a Sym watchtower destroyed by a lightning strike years ago. The tower's ruins are visible for miles and serve as a navigation landmark. It's said to be haunted by the soldiers who died there.

#### Encounter: The Deserter
*Social Encounter*

A young human man named Kellin is hiding in the ruins. He's a deserter from the Sym army—conscripted from a desert village, he fled after being ordered to beat a prisoner. He's injured (12 HP remaining, wounded leg) and desperate.

**Kellin's Knowledge:**
- Sym patrol schedules and weaknesses (disadvantage on Sym patrol encounter rolls for the next 2 days)
- The location of a hidden weapons cache (see below)
- Information about Chixie's defenses: "They've got the whole town locked down. Only miners go in and out, and they're searched. But there's an old drainage tunnel on the east side—might still be open."

**Complication:** A Sym patrol (4 **Scouts** and 1 **Veteran**) is tracking Kellin and will arrive in 1d4 hours. The party can hide, fight, or hand Kellin over.

**Weapons Cache:** In a buried crate near the tower's base (DC 15 Investigation to find): 6 **Silvered Shortswords**, 2 **Longbows** with 40 arrows, and 1 **Bag of Holding** containing 3 smoke bombs (as **Fog Cloud**, 1 charge each).

---

### Day 7: The Ashen Fields
**Distance:** 105 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** Recent wildfire damage, still smoldering

#### Environmental Challenge

A wildfire swept through this area three days ago (started by a lightning strike—possibly the same storm that hit the Spire). The ground is hot, the air thick with ash, and visibility is poor.

**Travel Mechanics:**
- Constitution saving throw DC 12 or gain 1 level of exhaustion from smoke inhalation
- Movement speed reduced by half due to debris and ash
- Perception checks have disadvantage beyond 60 feet

#### Encounter: The Fire Elemental
*Combat, CR 5*

The wildfire awakened a **Fire Elemental** that now wanders the burn zone, consuming the last flames. It's not inherently hostile but will attack if the party carries open flames or uses fire magic.

**Alternative:** A DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check reveals the elemental is confused and seeking "the heart of the fire." A **Control Flames** cantrip or similar magic can calm it, causing it to lead the party to a hidden cache:
- A metal strongbox that survived the fire
- Contains 150 gp, a **Scroll of Protection from Fire**, and mining maps showing a shortcut to Chixie (reduces travel time by 1 day)

---

### Day 7-8: The Mining Camps
**Distance:** 130 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** Industrialized hills, active mining operations

#### The Situation

The hills around Chixie are dotted with mining operations—some large and Sym-controlled, others small family claims. The party passes several as they approach the town. These camps are tense places: workers are watched by Sym overseers, and dissent is punished harshly.

#### Encounter: The Strikers
*Social/Moral Encounter*

At a medium-sized mining camp (run by the Vasquez family—the Tabaxi family mentioned in the background), the party encounters a strike. The workers (mostly human and Tabaxi) are refusing to enter the mines after a collapse killed three men. The overseer has called for Sym soldiers to break the strike.

**Key NPCs:**
- **Old Man Vasquez:** The mine owner, a Tabaxi caught between his workers and the Sym. He's fearful but not cruel—he's been forced to work with the occupiers to protect his family.
- **Marta (worker leader):** A young human woman whose brother died in the collapse. She's furious and willing to fight.
- **Overseer Kell:** A Sym-appointed human who's all too eager to call for military support.

**Resolution Options:**
- Mediate: Old Man Vasquez agrees to safety improvements if workers return; requires DC 16 Persuasion
- Sabotage: The party can disable the Sym communication equipment (DC 14 Thieves' Tools) to buy the workers time
- Side with workers: Fight the overseer and his guards (2 **Veterans**, 4 **Guards**); the workers will help but take casualties
- Side with management: Break the strike through intimidation; the workers will remember this

**Rewards for Helping Workers:**
- A grateful miner gives the party a map of the drainage tunnels beneath Chixie (crucial for the town infiltration)
- The workers will create distractions on the day of the rescue

**Consequence for Helping Management:**
- The party gains favor with Sym officials (easier to enter Chixie openly) but loses local support
- Fair Time's rescue becomes more difficult without insider help

---

### Day 8: Approaching Chixie
**Distance:** 150 miles from Silver
**Terrain:** Increasingly hilly, mining roads

#### The Final Approach

Chixie sits in a natural bowl surrounded by hills, its mine entrances visible from miles away. The town is walled, with checkpoints at all entrances. Smoke rises from the smelters, and the air tastes of coal dust.

**Sym Security:**
- 2 checkpoints on the main road (DC 15 Deception or bribery to pass with forged documents)
- 4 watch towers spaced around the walls (Perception checks vs. party Stealth to avoid)
- Patrols circling the perimeter every 30 minutes

**Seymour's Plan:** His caravan has legitimate business at the mines, so he can get the party to the outer mining camp. From there, they'll need to find their own way into the town proper. He suggests:
1. The drainage tunnel (if the party obtained the map)
2. Hiding in a coal cart (DC 13 Stealth, but subjects to random search)
3. Bribing a guard at the eastern gate (100 gp, requires a contact inside)

**If the Party Arrives After Day 7:** Fair Time is already being prepared for transport. The rescue becomes a moving target—they'll need to intercept the convoy on the road north, fighting a significantly larger force (2 **Knights**, 6 **Veterans**, 1 **Mage**).

## Towns and Waystations Along the Route

### Silver (Starting Point)
*Population: ~5,000*

The regional capital of western The Sym, Silver is built around an ancient steel forge that has operated for centuries. The town's name comes from the appearance of the forge's glow on the surrounding hills at night. Currently under Sym occupation, with martial law in effect.

**Notable Locations:**
- **The Steel Quarter:** Industrial district with the great forge at its center
- **Pale Sun's Gambling House:** A neutral meeting ground for all factions
- **The Shrine of Ten Thousand Steps:** A pre-Sym religious site now under military guard

**Sym Presence:** Heavy (200+ soldiers, 1 **Mage**, multiple **Knights**)

### Vashti's Rest (Day 3-4)
*Population: ~200*

A waystation settlement built around a natural spring. The town is a crucial resupply point for travelers and a hub of quiet resistance activity.

### The Mining Camps (Day 7-8)
*Population: ~500 total across multiple camps*

Scattered settlements around Chixie's mining operations. The largest is run by the Vasquez family, but several smaller concerns are operated by independent prospectors. All are under Sym oversight.

### Chixie (Destination)
*Population: ~3,000*

A mining town built around rich coal and iron deposits in the central hills. Currently under lockdown by Sym forces, with all non-mining activity suspended. Fair Time is held in the Temple of the First Flame, guarded by elite soldiers.

**Sym Presence:** Very heavy (150 soldiers, 2 **Mages**, multiple **Knights**, 1 **War Priest**)

## Story Hooks and Complications

1. **The Pale Sun Connection:** First Sun's scuffle at the gambling house was staged to identify potential allies. If the party intervened, she secretly passes them a note with useful information about Sym patrol routes.

2. **The Wendigo's Curse:** If the party failed to properly deal with the wendigos (or didn't take the charms), one party member begins having nightmares of hunger. After three nights, they gain a craving for raw meat (roleplay effect; DC 13 Wisdom save to resist eating uncooked meat when offered).

3. **The Strike's Ripple Effects:** How the party handles the mining strike affects their options in Chixie. Worker support means access to tunnels and distractions; worker animosity means increased difficulty finding local allies.

4. **Fair Time's Health:** The religious elder is not in good condition. Reports suggest he's been drugged to keep him compliant during the upcoming transport. Healing magic will be needed to move him quickly.

## Expanding the Journey

This outline covers the main Winding Way route. For additional content, consider:

- **Side Quests:** Ancient ruins with pre-Sym artifacts, wildlife disturbances, missing persons
- **Weather Events:** Flash floods, dust storms, extreme heat
- **Sym Operations:** Weapons testing, forced labor camps, propaganda broadcasts
- **Local Legends:** Ghost stories, treasure maps, supernatural occurrences
- **Rival Groups:** Other smugglers, bounty hunters, desperate refugees

The journey from Silver to Chixie should feel like a living world where the party's choices matter. Every encounter listed here can be adjusted for difficulty based on party composition and player preferences.
