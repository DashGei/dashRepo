# Under-Boat Crew & Transmuter Mission Leader: Encounter Play-Out

## Overview

The under-boat is a Diamond Teeth vessel—a sleek, steam-and-magic-powered submersible designed for covert operations and equipment transport. It crashed deliberately (Habel's doing), but the crew survived. They are now desperate to recapture Habel and Lathos, recover any sensitive materials, and eliminate witnesses. The mission leader is a transmuter wizard who was overseeing the transport of Lathos and the ship's valuable cargo.

**Party Assumption:** Level 5–6, four PCs (Innenza, Nibin, Anadine, Callahan), plus Habel and Lathos as allied NPCs (use **Scout** and **Apprentice** stat blocks if needed, or keep them as non-combatants who assist via skills).

**Encounter Difficulty:** Hard to Deadly if the party fights the entire crew at once. Better to run it in **waves** as crew members emerge from the wreckage and organize. The transmuter leader should be a **boss-level threat** for this tier—CR 6–7.

## The Mission Leader: Vaelis Thorn

**Role:** Pilot, mission commander, transmuter wizard  
**Appearance:** A tall, gaunt sea elf with ink-stained fingers, a shaved head, and alchemical burns on his forearms. He wears a reinforced leather coat with Diamond Teeth insignia and carries a staff topped with a floating, slowly rotating crystal.  
**Personality:** Cold, calculating, and utterly pragmatic. He views the crash as an inconvenience, not a disaster. He has no loyalty to Habel—she was a tool—and sees Lathos as stolen property. He is *fascinated* by Innenza's ring and will prioritize capturing her alive if he notices it.  
**Motivation:** Recover Lathos, kill or capture Habel, and salvage the ship's cargo (which includes rare transmutation reagents). He will retreat if the fight turns against him, preferring to report back to Ahmrose than die in a ruin.

## Encounter Structure: The Library Pursuit

The under-boat has crashed into the ruins adjacent to the library. The crew emerges in **three waves**, giving the party time to react and Habel/Lathos to join the fight.

### Wave 1: The Crash Site (Round 1–2)
- **Krell** and **two guards (Orin & Tella)** emerge from the wreckage, weapons drawn. They demand the party surrender Habel and Lathos.
- **Sessa** follows, carrying a toolbox and looking conflicted.
- **Vaelis** stays near the ship, observing. He casts *mirror image* on himself (Round 1) and *slow* on the party's biggest threat (Round 2).

### Wave 2: The Organizer (Round 3–4)
- **Phira** and **Voss** emerge, having secured the ship's cargo.
- Phira casts *fog cloud* to cover the crew's advance. Voss uses *bless* on Krell and the guards.
- Vaelis uses *telekinesis* to drop a bookshelf on a PC (DC 15 Dex save or 3d6 bludgeoning and restrained).

### Wave 3: The Leader's Gambit (Round 5+)
- If the crew is losing, Vaelis casts *haste* on Krell and *greater invisibility* on himself, attempting to grab Lathos and *misty step* away.
- If Vaelis is reduced below 30 HP, he casts *polymorph* into a giant octopus (or *blink* + *expeditious retreat*) and flees into the water, abandoning the crew.
- If Krell falls, the remaining crew (except Voss) fight to the death. Voss surrenders.

### Environmental Hazards
- **Collapsing Shelves:** A PC can use an action to topple a shelf (DC 13 Athletics). Creatures in a 10-ft. line must make a DC 13 Dex save or take 2d6 bludgeoning and be restrained.
- **Steam Vents:** Ruptured pipes create 5-ft. squares of scalding steam (DC 12 Con save or 1d6 fire damage at the start of turn).
- **Unstable Floor:** Parts of the library are flooded; difficult terrain. A *thunderwave* or similar spell could collapse the floor entirely (DM discretion).
- **Magical Books:** A stray spell might activate a random book (roll on a minor wild magic table or have it cast *grease* or *silent image*).

- **Transmutation Engine:** The ship's power source is a **large cut ruby** (roughly 6 inches across, worth 5,000 gp) housed in a brass and glass containment unit in the engine room. The ruby radiates transmutation magic and is the ship's "heart." Without it, the ship cannot move or power its systems.
- **Steam Propulsion:** Secondary system using pressurized steam; can move the ship at 10 ft. for 1 hour if the ruby is removed, but requires constant refueling and a DC 15 Tinker's Tools check to operate.
- **Observation Dome:** Glass bubble amidships; can be used as a vantage point or emergency exit. Currently cracked (disadvantage on Perception checks through it).

### Current Damage (Repair Needed)
To make the ship operational, the party must complete a **repair skill challenge** (4 successes before 3 failures) using Athletics, Investigation, Tinker's Tools, or Arcana. Habel has advantage on Tinker's Tools checks. Each check takes 10 minutes. On success, the ship's HP is restored to 80 and it can move at 30 ft. swimming. Full repair (120 HP) requires a long rest and additional materials (or a trip to Ko'ar Tai).

**Complications during repair:**
- Diamond Teeth patrols (1-in-4 chance per check)
- Unstable ruins (DC 12 Dex save or take 1d6 bludgeoning from falling debris)
- The transmutation engine sparks dangerously (DC 13 Arcana or take 1d8 force damage)

## The Ruby Power Core

### Description
The ruby is a flawless, deep crimson gem the size of a fist, cut with dozens of facets that catch the light and refract it into faint red beams. It sits in a **containment cradle**—a brass frame with copper coils and glass panels—bolted to the engine room floor. When active, the ruby pulses with a slow, rhythmic glow, and the air around it hums with transmutation magic. It is **warm to the touch** and smells faintly of ozone.

**Arcana DC 15 (or Habel's passive knowledge):** This ruby is not just a power source—it is a **transmutation focus**, likely harvested from a powerful elemental or conjured entity. It has been modified to interface with the ship's engine. Removing it without proper precautions could cause a feedback surge.

### Why Remove It?
- The party needs **three cut rubies** for the Atlantean teleporter. This is one of them.
- The ruby is valuable (5,000 gp) and could be sold or traded in Ko'ar Tai.
- Removing it disables the ship, preventing the Diamond Teeth from pursuing (if they haven't already been dealt with).
- Lathos believes the ruby's transmutation energy could help stabilize Innenza's ring (long shot, but a plot hook).

### Removal Procedure

Removing the ruby is a **multi-step process** that requires time, skill, and a steady hand. It cannot be done casually mid-combat without significant risk.

#### Step 1: Access the Engine Room
The engine room is amidships, below deck. The door is **locked** (DC 15 Thieves' Tools) but Habel has the key (she's the head mechanic). During the crash, the door is **jammed** (DC 13 Athletics to force open) or can be bypassed with a DC 14 Investigation check to find the emergency release.

#### Step 2: Shut Down the Engine
The engine must be **powered down** before the ruby can be safely removed. This requires a **DC 15 Tinker's Tools** check (Habel has advantage) or a **DC 16 Arcana** check to safely disengage the magical flow. **Time:** 1 minute (10 rounds).  
- **Failure:** The engine sputters but doesn't shut down. You can try again, but each failure increases the DC by 1 (max DC 18).  
- **Critical Failure (by 5 or more):** A feedback surge deals 2d6 force damage to everyone in the engine room (DC 13 Dex save for half) and alerts all remaining crew to your location.

#### Step 3: Disengage the Containment Cradle
Once the engine is off, the ruby's glow fades to a faint ember. The cradle has **three locking clamps** that must be released in sequence. This requires a **DC 14 Sleight of Hand** check (or **DC 14 Tinker's Tools**) to avoid damaging the ruby. **Time:** 1 round per clamp (3 rounds total).  
- **Failure:** One clamp jams. You must spend an action and a **DC 12 Strength (Athletics)** check to force it, but doing so cracks the ruby's housing—the ruby itself is unharmed, but the cradle is ruined (can't be reused).

#### Step 4: Extract the Ruby
With the clamps released, the ruby can be lifted free. It is **hot** (1d4 fire damage to bare hands, avoidable with gloves or a tool). A **DC 10 Arcana** check identifies that the ruby is still "charged" and should be wrapped in insulating material (cloth, leather, or a lead-lined box) to prevent accidental discharge. **Time:** 1 action.

#### Step 5: Stabilize the Engine (Optional)
If the party wants to prevent the ship from exploding or leaking transmutation energy, they must **cap the empty cradle** with a **DC 13 Tinker's Tools** check. **Time:** 1 minute.  
- **Failure:** The engine room becomes a **wild magic zone** for 1 hour (any spell cast in the engine room triggers a wild magic surge on a d20 roll of 1–3).

### Total Time
- **Rushed (combat):** 5 rounds (30 seconds) if all checks are made, but each check is made at **disadvantage** and failure triggers a surge.
- **Careful (out of combat):** 5–10 minutes total.

### Risks & Consequences
- **Noise:** The shutdown process is loud (whining metal, hissing steam). Any remaining Diamond Teeth crew will investigate.
- **Magical Signature:** The ruby radiates strong transmutation magic. If Vaelis is still alive and nearby, he will sense the engine shutting down (Arcana check opposed by Vaelis's passive Arcana 17).
- **The Ruby's Nature:** If the party examines the ruby (Arcana DC 16), they learn it was created by **fusing a water elemental with a cut gemstone**—a process Vaelis specializes in. This is a clue to his transmutation skills and may be relevant to Lathos's research.

## Integration with the Encounter

### During the Fight
- **Vaelis's Priority:** If he sees anyone heading toward the engine room, he will cast *slow* or *telekinesis* to stop them. He knows the ruby is valuable and will not let it be stolen.
- **Habel's Knowledge:** Habel can explain the removal process in **1 round** (free action) if asked, but she warns: *"If you pull that ruby while the engine's hot, we're all going to have a bad time."*
- **Sessa's Sabotage:** If Sessa (the mechanic) is still alive and hostile, she may try to **overload the engine** as a last resort (DC 15 Tinker's Tools, 3 rounds). If successful, the ship explodes (6d6 fire damage in a 30-ft. radius, DC 15 Dex save for half). The party can stop her with a successful Intimidation or Persuasion check (DC 14).

### After the Fight
- If the party removes the ruby, they have **one of the three needed for the teleporter**. They now have **two rubies** (assuming they still have the first one).
- The ship is disabled. If the party wants to use it later, they'll need to find a replacement power source (another large gemstone, a powerful elemental, or a favor from a wizard in Ko'ar Tai).
- Habel is **not happy** about gutting her ship, but she understands the necessity: *"I can rig a steam bypass, but it'll be slow. We're not outrunning anyone in this."*

### Optional: The Ruby's Secret
If the party attunes to the ruby (requires a short rest and a DC 15 Arcana check), they discover it contains a **fragment of a trapped water elemental's consciousness**. It whispers in Aquan: *"Free me... free me..."* This is a plot hook for later—the elemental could be an ally, a quest-giver, or a source of information about Vaelis's experiments.
