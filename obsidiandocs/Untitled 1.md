## Martial Maneuvers

Martial Maneuvers represent specialized techniques, combat maneuvers, and trained abilities that martial characters can perform in battle.

Unlike spells, Martial Maneuvers are fueled by Maneuver Slots. You expend Maneuver Slots to activate powerful abilities and regain all expended Maneuver Slots when you finish a Short Rest.

Martial Maneuvers are divided into four tiers. As you gain levels, you unlock access to more powerful Maneuvers and gain additional Maneuver Slots to fuel them.

## Maneuver Slots

Maneuver Slots represent your endurance, training, and ability to perform exceptional techniques under pressure.

Whenever you activate a Maneuver you expend one of your available slots. You regain all expended Maneuver Slots when you finish a Short Rest.

## Learning Maneuvers

As you gain levels, you learn Martial Maneuvers from your class Maneuver list.

Each Maneuver belongs to one of four tiers.


| Tier   | Level Required |
| ------ | -------------- |
| Tier 1 | 2nd            |
| Tier 2 | 5th            |
| Tier 3 | 11th           |
| Tier 4 | 17th           |

Whenever you prepare a new Maneuver, you may choose any Maneuver from a tier you have access to.

Some class Maneuverures, subclasses, and Fighting Styles grant additional Maneuvers. Unless stated otherwise, these Maneuvers do not count against your Maneuvers Prepared.

## Using a Maneuver

Each Maneuver describes when and how it can be activated.

A Maneuver may require an Action, a Reaction, an attack, or another trigger. If a Maneuver does not specify an Action cost, activating it requires no Action, Reaction, or Bonus Action.

Unless a Maneuver states otherwise, activating it costs one Maneuver Slot.

### One Maneuver Per Turn

You cannot activate more than one Maneuver during your turn.

Maneuvers activated as Reactions on your own turn or another creature's turn do not count toward this limit.

### Maneuver Saving Throws

Some Maneuvers require a target to make a saving throw.

When a Maneuver calls for a saving throw, it uses either your Physical DC or your Mental DC, as specified in the Maneuver's description.

A Maneuver that lists Physical DC Strength means the target makes a Strength saving throw against your Physical DC.

A Maneuver that lists Mental DC Wisdom means the target makes a Wisdom saving throw against your Mental DC.

All expended Maneuver Slots are regained when you finish a Short Rest.

# Underdog Maneuvers, Tier 1

## Blind Strike

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit a with an attack. The target makes a Physical DC Constitution Save. On a failure it is Blinded until the start of your next turn.

## Bolster

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver when you take the Dodge Action. You gain Advantage on Strength and Constitution effects instead of Dexterity for the duration of the Dodge.

## Camouflage Netting

**Exploration, Combat, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver and spend 10 minutes setting up a 15 foot cube of netting and material. The area inside is Heavily Obscured to outsiders.

## Defensive Positions

**Exploration, Combat, FTR**

Activate this Maneuver and spend 10 minutes setting up a 15 foot cube of defensive structures.  
A creature inside has Half-Cover against outside creatures, and Advantage against Dexterity Saves from effects originating outside of the area.  
When a creature inside the structure hits a creature outside of area, they can choose to push the target 10 feet away from the position.

## Disarm

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit a with an attack. The target makes a Physical DC Strength Save. On a failure it drops one item that it was holding, which lands at its feet.

## Feint

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you make a melee attack. The attack has an additional 5 feet of range, and you do not provoke reactions from the target until the start of your next turn.

## Overpowering Strike

Activate this Maneuver when you make an attack. Instead of rolling to hit, the target makes a Physical DC Dexterity Save. On a failure it is hit by the attack, on a success it takes 1d6 Bludgeoning damage.

## Parry

**Combat, Physical. FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

When a creature makes an attack roll against you, make an attack roll with a held weapon as a Reaction . If your attack roll exceeds or equals theirs, their attack misses.

## Precision Attack

**Combat, Physical, FTR, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit a with a melee attack. Add a d4 to the attack roll, possibly causing the attack to hit.

## Reinforce

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action. A creature of your choice within 10 feet has Advantage on their next D20 test and gains 1d4 Temporary Hit Points equal to your level. The creature can choose to spend any of these Temporary Hit Points on the D20 Test you gave them Advantage on, but they lose the Hit Points.

## Riposte

**Combat, Physical. FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

When a creature misses you with an attack, you can Activate this Maneuver as a Reaction, to make a melee attack against the creature.

## Startle

**Combat, Social, Mental, BAR, FTR, MNK, RGR**

Activate this Maneuver as a Bonus Action to Intimidate a creature of your choice who can hear you within 30 feet. The target makes a Mental DC Wisdom Save. On a failure it is Frightened of you until the start of your next turn.  
When out of combat, they are Frightened for a minute, and you have Advantage on Intimidation Checks against that creature while they are Frightened.

## Trip

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit with an attack. The target must make a Physical DC Strength Save. On a failure, it falls prone.

# Heroic Maneuvers. Tier 2

## Blade Dance

Activate this Maneuver as an Action. For each unique adjacent square you occupy during your turn next to an enemy creature, that creature takes 1d4 physical damage at the end of your turn, taking half that damage if they succeed a Physical DC Dexterity saving throw.

## Boring

**Social, Mental, FTR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver and monologue for a minute. After which one non-hostile creature of your choosing makes a Mental DC Wisdom Save. On failure the target dozes off and falls unconscious for an hour or until woken.

## Chokeout

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

When you have a creature grappled, you can activate this Maneuver as an Action. The grappled target makes a Physical DC Strength Save. On failure it is choking (as in the hazard) for as long as it is grappled by you. If you inflict all 6 levels of Exhaustion this way, you can choose to let the creature fall unconscious for 8 hours instead of them dying.

While choking a creature, both hands are occupied and you cannot take Reactions, Bonus Actions, or Actions. If you take damage, the target can repeat the grapple Save.

## Coup de Grâce

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit a creature with an attack. If the attack deals more than 75% of the target's hitpoint maximum, the target must make a Physical DC Constitution Save. On failure, the creature is reduced to 0 hitpoints.

## Cover Fire

**Combat, Physical, FTR, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action and focus your attention in a 30 foot cone in front of you. When an enemy creature makes an attack or moves within the cone you can make a ranged attack against that creature as a Reaction.  
A creature can avoid this reaction by choosing to make attack rolls at Disadvantage, or treating the cone as difficult terrain.  
The cone moves with you and you may change the direction when you start your turn.

## Daggerpush

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action to make a weapon attack against a prone creature within 5 feet of you. You also become prone, and the target makes a Physical DC Strength Save as you push your weapon into them. On failure the attack roll is a Critical Hit.

## Deduce

**Exploration, Mental, Social, MNK, ROG**

When you make an Investigation Check on something which has already happened, a History Check, or when hearing a story told to you, you can activate this Maneuver. The DM then gives you a third person playback of what you have learned as if you were a fly on the wall. If it was a check, you add 1d10 to the roll and the scene gives you appropriate information for the new roll as determined by the DM.

## Excuse Me

**Social, Mental, MNK, ROG**

When a creature within 30 feet of you fails a Charisma check, you can activate this Maneuver as a Reaction in order to hopefully save face. After making your interjection, add 1d10 to the previous roll, possibly turning failure into success.

## Fleeting

**Social, Mental, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an action and choose a non-hostile creature you have interacted with for at most 5 minutes. The target makes a Mental DC Intelligence Save. On failure it entirely forgets your name, voice, and face.

## Hurl

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver when you take the Attack Action. You can choose to use a grappled creature as the weapon for the attack. Make a ranged attack (STR+PB) against a creature you can see within 30 feet. Both creatures take 2d6+STR Bludgeoning damage.  
You can also simply jettison the creature up to 30 feet in a direction of your choosing instead of making an attack roll.

## Intuition

**Social, Mental, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Spend 1 minute in deep thought to activate this Maneuver. Choose a course of action, the DM tells you whether or not they believe next the next hour following that action will be kind to the party or not.

## Nod

**Social, Mental, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action to make a vague gesture to a creature who can see you. That creature receives a 5 word message composed by you.

## Ricochet

**Combat, Physical, FTR, RGR, ROG**

Activate this as an Action to make a ranged weapon attack against a creature. This attack ignores all cover, provided that there is a surface for the projectile to bounce off of. When determining range, use the distance to and from the bounced surface.

## Silver Tongue

**Social, Mental, FTR, ROG, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action when speaking to a creature who can hear you. The target must make a Mental DC Wisdom Save. On failure it is Charmed by you for the next 10 minutes. This Charm effect is not magical, and simply sets the target's attitude towards you to Friendly while Charmed. Acting in a way which would make any Friendly creature unfriendly ends the Charm.

## Stealth Tactics

**Exploration, Physical, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action to move your party silently and without trace. For the next 10 minutes, all creatures within the aura add your Stealth bonus instead of their own when making Stealth Checks.

## Sweet Spot

**Combat, Physical, FTR, RGR, ROG**

When a creature ends their turn within 30-40 feet from you, you can activate this Maneuver as a Reaction and make a ranged weapon attack against the creature.

When you finish a rest, you can swap the range to any 10 foot range between 10-150 feet.

## Repositioning Shove

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit a creature with a melee attack. The creature makes a Physical DC Strength Save. On failure it is pushed up to 10 feet in any direction. Your speed then increases by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

## Vicious Mockery

**Combat, Mental, BAR, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action to hurl an insult at a creature who can hear you. The target takes 1d4 Psychic damage and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll if the target is not you.

# Epic Maneuvers, Tier 3

## Counter Stance

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action and assume an intricate stance designed to flip enemy maneuvers. Until the start of your next turn, when a creature attempts to grapple, move you, or knock you prone, it must first make Physical DC Strength Save. On a failure you Grapple it, knock it prone, or push it 10 feet in a direction of your choice.

## Grand Guardian

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR**

When you are subject to damage, a Constitution Save, a Dexterity Save, or a Strength Save as part of an area of effect, you can activate this Maneuver as a Reaction to hold up a shield or weapon with the Heavy property. a 10 foot cone then extends facing away from the origin point of the area. Everything in that cone has Resistance to the damage, and has Advantage on Saves forced by the area.

The cone lasts until the start of your next turn, but you can continue holding the equipment as an Action, which extends the duration of the cone until the start of your next turn or until you stop spending Actions to extend the effect.

## Flee Cowards

**Combat, Mental, BAR, FTR**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action and bellow forth a mighty cry in a 30 foot cone. Enemy creatures in the cone must succeed Mental DC Wisdom Saves or be Frightened of you until the start of your next turn and must immediately use their Reaction to move up to their speed away from you.

## Pinned

**Combat, Physical, FTR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you hit a creature with an attack dealing Piercing or Slashing damage, you pin the weapon or ammunition through them into a nearby surface. Until the creature uses their Action to remove the pin, their speed is always 0.

## Power Shot

Activate this Maneuver as an Action with a ranged attack. Creatures in straight line out to the weapon's normal range must make a Physical DC Dexterity saving throw. On a failure creatures take damage as if they were hit by a weapon attack + 2d6 Thunder damage.

## Spell Splitter

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK**

When a spellcaster casts and spell and you are within 5 feet of the target of the spell, you can activate this Maneuver as a Reaction and make an attack. If the attack roll exceeds the target's Spell DC, the spell dissipates with no effect. If the spell's level is higher than your proficiency bonus, the attack roll has Disadvantage.

## Take it and Leave it

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, ROG**

When you Critically hit with a weapon dealing Piercing or Slashing damage, you can choose to leave the weapon in the target instead of dealing extra damage. Once per turn when the target makes a D20 Test, it must choose to either takes the weapon's damage, or subtract the weapon's damage dice from the D20 Test. The creature can use their Action to remove the weapon, taking the weapon's damage dice in the process

## Vengeful Strike

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as Reaction when hit with a Critical Hit. Make an attack against the attacker, this attack is automatically a Critical Hit.

## Zephyr Slash

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action and flourish a melee weapon. Afterwards up instantly move up to 15 feet in a line, forcing all creatures you pass through to make a Physical DC Dexterity saving throw. Creatures take damage as if they were hit by one weapon attack with on a failure, and half as much damage on a success. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

# Legendary Maneuvers, Tier 4

***These Maneuvers are so powerful and complex that one may only be acted out once per long rest. This suspends the use of any Tier 3 or 4 Maneuvers.***

## Adamantine Assault

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver and take the attack Action, targeting a non-magical object. The object immediately shatters and is broken.

## Contingency Plane

**Mental, FTR, ROG**

As an Action, you can use this Maneuver to reveal a previously undisclosed plan (which you make up at the time of its reveal) and make a DC 20 Intelligence check. You may add a skill the DM deems appropriate to your plan. On a success, your secret plans and preparations take effect perfectly as you described them. The cost to execute the plan cannot exceed 5,000 gold, and the effects should not exceed those of a 7th-level spell. For example, you might reveal that you secretly purchased a perfect spell scroll last time you were in a town, or that you paid a mercenary band or airship to come to your rescue.

## Perfect Execution

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver before making an attack roll. You attack is automatically a Critical Hit.

## Rally the Fallen

**Combat, Mental, BAR, FTR**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action and call out to your injured allies. Each ally within 60 feet that is at 0 hit points but not dead regains 6d12 hit points. An ally who died within the last 10 minutes can make a DC 15 Constitution Save to spontaneously will themselves back to life with 1d4 Exhaustion levels, unless their body was destroyed.

## Sandevistan's Special Technique

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver when you start your turn. For the rest of the turn, time for everyone and everything else stands still. Creatures other than you cannot take Reactions or use features, and all of your attacks automatically hit.

## Steel Wind Strike

**Combat, Physical, FTR, MNK, RGR, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action, choose up to five creatures 30 feet. Make an attack against each target. On a hit, a target takes the attacks damage and loses their Reaction. You can then teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 5 feet of one of the targets you hit or missed.

## Thunderclap

**Combat, Physical, BAR, FTR, MNK**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action to create a devastating shockwave that emanates in either a 50 foot cone, or a 20 foot circle. Each creature of your choice in the area makes a Physical DC Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 8d10 Thunder damage and has the Prone condition. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

## Unfathomable Movement

**Exploration, Physical, MNK, ROG**

Activate this Maneuver as an Action when you are not being observed by any other creatures. Somehow, you manage to (Teleport) get to an unoccupied space you know of within 30 feet.
