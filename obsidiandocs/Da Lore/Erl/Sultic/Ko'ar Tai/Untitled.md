# Ko'ar Tai: The City in the Trench

## Overview

Ko'ar Tai is the largest city on Gaia—a vertical metropolis of 4.5 million souls built across and down the walls of a massive marine trench. Above, mostly uninhabited volcanic islands rise from the sea, their peaks shrouded in steam and ash. Below, the trench plunges into darkness, its walls veined with metal ore and its depths lit by the orange glow of smelters.

The city is Victorian in spirit: ironwork and brass, gas lamps (alchemical and bioluminescent), steam-driven machinery, rigid class distinctions, and a culture of manners and appearances. But it is also aquatic—every street is a current, every building is pressurized, and every citizen knows how to move in three dimensions. Sea elves, tritons, dragonborn, and water genasi make up the bulk of the population, with surface traders and deep-dwelling emissaries adding to the mix.

Four families rule Ko'ar Tai, each controlling a territory, an industry, and a private army. Their compounds are fortresses, their names are brands, and their feuds are the city's lifeblood.

## The Vertical City: How Ko'ar Tai Works

The city is built in **five vertical tiers**, each with its own character:

### Tier 1: The Surface Isles

The volcanic islands above the trench. Docks for surface trade, air-breathing embassies, and the estates of those wealthy enough to afford open sky. Steam vents power the lifts that connect to the upper trench. The air here is sulfurous and warm. **Who's here:** Rockmund family (partially), foreign merchants, surface colony representatives. **Sensory:** Sunlight (filtered through ash clouds), the cry of seabirds, the clank of cargo cranes, the smell of tar and volcanic gas.

### Tier 2: The Upper Trench (The Ledge)

The wealthiest part of the city, clinging to the trench walls just below the surface. Gas lamps line promenades. Iron-and-glass buildings with pressurized balconies overlook the drop. Private submersibles dock at personal moorings. **Who's here:** Rockmund territory (business, high income), Garren family compound, foreign banks, luxury shops. **Sensory:** Warm currents, the hum of private generators, the scent of imported tea and perfume, the distant roar of the smelters below.

### Tier 3: The Mid-Trench (The Works)

The industrial heart. Factories, workshops, and dense residential blocks. Steam pipes crisscross the streets like veins. The water here is warmer, tinged with soot. Shift whistles mark the hours. **Who's here:** Elwei territory (manufacturing), Diamond Teeth patrols, working-class neighborhoods, the Grand Market. **Sensory:** Clanging metal, hissing steam, the taste of iron in the water, the constant thrum of machinery.

### Tier 4: The Lower Trench (The Veins)

The mining district. Tunnels branch off from the main trench into the rock, following ore veins. Smelters glow in the dark. Cave-ins are common. The water is hot and mineral-rich. **Who's here:** Blaang territory (mining), miners' unions, the desperate and the indebted. **Sensory:** Orange light from furnaces, the rumble of drills, the smell of sulfur and hot metal, the pressure of depth.

### Tier 5: The Deep Vents (The Sump)

The bottom of the trench. Superheated water rises from volcanic vents. Kelp farms (manatee pastures) float in the warmer currents. The poorest citizens live here, in shanties built around vent chimneys. **Who's here:** Kelp territory (food production, partially), the destitute, outcasts, and those hiding from the families. **Sensory:** Boiling water, bioluminescent glow, the low moan of tectonic pressure, the taste of salt and minerals.

**Transport:** Lifts (steam-powered cages) connect the tiers. Current-chutes (controlled water flows) allow fast travel downward. Personal submersibles are common among the wealthy; the poor use public ferries or swim. The **Trenchspine**—a massive iron column driven into the trench wall—serves as the central hub, with bridges and platforms radiating outward.

## The Four Territories

### Blaang Territory (43% of the city) — Mining

**Location:** Tier 4 and the upper parts of Tier 5. The largest territory by area, sprawling through tunnels and caverns carved into the trench walls.  
**Primary Industry:** Mining—iron, copper, silver, gold, and rare magical ores. Smelting and initial processing happen here before the metal is sent up to Elwei for manufacturing.

**Character:** Blaang territory is a frontier. It's loud, dangerous, and full of opportunity. The Blaang family's sigil—a pickaxe crossed with a gem—is everywhere, but the family's grip is slipping. Since Reven's death, the Diamond Teeth have moved in, offering "protection" and slowly taking over the mining operations. Miners are divided: some pay the Diamond Teeth for safety, others resist, and many just want to work.

**Sights:**

- **The Grand Shaft:** The main entrance to the mines, a massive vertical tunnel with platforms descending into darkness. Steam lifts carry miners up and down in shifts.
    
- **The Smelters:** Caverns filled with glowing furnaces and channels of molten metal. The heat is intense; visitors need pressure suits or magic to endure it.
    
- **The Union Hall:** A defiant, battered building where miners organize. The Blaang family tolerates it (barely); the Diamond Teeth want it shut down.
    
- **Reven's Memorial:** A small shrine at the site of the accident that killed the Blaang heir. Someone keeps leaving fresh flowers—and someone keeps knocking them down.

**Sounds:** Drills, hammers, the roar of furnaces, the occasional cave-in siren. **Smells:** Sulfur, hot metal, sweat, and the sharp tang of ozone. **People:** Miners (mostly tritons and dragonborn), union organizers, Diamond Teeth enforcers, desperate families.

**DM Hooks:**

- A miner finds a strange gem that whispers to them. It's a fragment of Sorin's ring—or something like it.
    
- The Diamond Teeth are pressuring a mine foreman to sign over his claim. The party can intervene.
    
- Zeke Blaang hasn't been seen in weeks. The estate is closed. His courtesans come and go, but no one else.


---

### Elwei Territory (29% of the city) — Manufacturing

**Location:** Tier 3, the Mid-Trench. Factories and workshops dominate the skyline, their smokestacks venting steam and alchemical byproducts into the current.  
**Primary Industry:** Manufacturing—weapons, tools, machinery, steam engines, and firearms. The Elwei name is stamped on everything from mining drills to luxury pocket watches.

**Character:** Elwei territory is the engine of Ko'ar Tai. It's crowded, efficient, and ruthless. Ahmrose Elwei's Diamond Teeth run security here, and their presence is felt everywhere: checkpoints, patrols, and the ever-present threat of "labor disputes" being resolved by mercenaries. The Elwei family's sigil—a hammer striking a diamond—is painted on factory walls, but the Diamond Teeth's actual emblem (a diamond with teeth) is just as common.

**Sights:**

- **The Forgeworks:** The largest factory complex, a labyrinth of assembly lines, steam presses, and alchemical vats. It runs day and night.
    
- **The Diamond Teeth Compound:** A fortified headquarters in the center of the territory. Ahmrose's office is here, along with the teleportation circle the party may have arrived through.
    
- **The Worker's Row:** Dense residential blocks where factory workers live. Tenements, taverns, and cheap food stalls. Rumors spread fast here.
    
- **The Garren-Rockmund-Zalthbash Office:** The engineering conglomerate's local headquarters. Habel's father, Jacel, has an office here—though he rarely visits.
    

**Sounds:** Steam whistles, grinding gears, shouting foremen, the hiss of cooling metal. **Smells:** Oil, coal smoke, hot brass, and the bitter tang of alchemical runoff. **People:** Factory workers (sea elves and water genasi mostly), Diamond Teeth mercenaries, engineers, foremen, and union organizers (carefully watched).

**DM Hooks:**

- A factory is sabotaged—or is it? The Diamond Teeth blame a union organizer, but the evidence doesn't add up.
    
- Habel's father, Jacel, is in the city. He's been meeting with Kethy Rockmund. Why?
    
- The Diamond Teeth are recruiting aggressively. A young worker is offered a place in their ranks—and doesn't know it's a death sentence.

### Rockmund Territory (7% of the city) — Business and High Income

**Location:** Tier 2, the Upper Trench, with estates on the surface isles. The smallest territory by area, but the wealthiest per capita.  
**Primary Industry:** Business, finance, international trade, and high-income services. Kethy Rockmund, an international businessman, heads the family. The Rockmund name is synonymous with banking, insurance, and the engineering conglomerate Garren, Rockmund, and Zalthbash.

**Character:** Rockmund territory is polished and quiet. Glass-domed promenades, private gardens, and offices with brass nameplates. The water here is clean and warm. Security is discreet but omnipresent—private guards, not family thugs. The Rockmund family's sigil is a set of scales balancing a gem and a coin. They are neutral in the family feuds, but their neutrality is profitable.

**Sights:**

- **The Grand Exchange:** A massive glass-and-iron building where metals, goods, and contracts are traded. The heartbeat of Ko'ar Tai's economy.
    
- **The Rockmund Bank:** A fortress of marble and steel, built into the trench wall. Vaults extend deep into the rock.
    
- **The Promenade:** A curved street of luxury shops, tea houses, and private clubs. Gas lamps flicker with alchemical light.
    
- **Kethy Rockmund's Estate:** A surface isle mansion with a private submersible dock and a view of the volcanic peaks.


**Sounds:** Quiet conversations, the clink of glasses, the hum of private generators, the distant echo of the city below. **Smells:** Imported tea, polished wood, salt air, and the faint scent of ozone from the engineering offices. **People:** Bankers, merchants, diplomats, engineers, and the wealthy elite. Few laborers—they're not welcome here.

**DM Hooks:**

- Kethy Rockmund is looking for information about the Diamond Teeth's expansion. He'll pay well—or trade favors.
    
- A Rockmund banker is laundering money for Ahmrose. The party could expose this and destabilize the Diamond Teeth's funding.
    
- Habel's father, Jacel Garren, is staying at a Rockmund guest house. He's paranoid, heavily guarded, and desperate to find his daughter.

### Kelp Territory (21% of the city) — Food and Imports

**Location:** Tier 5 (the deep vents) and the surface docks. The Kelp family controls the manatee farms in the warm currents and the import docks where surface goods arrive.  
**Primary Industry:** Food production (manatee farming, kelp cultivation) and imports (surface goods, luxury items, and foreign technology).

**Character:** Kelp territory is the city's stomach. It's busy, practical, and less formal than the others. The Kelp family's sigil—a manatee curled around a stalk of kelp—marks every market stall and warehouse. The family is furious about Reven Blaang's death; the marriage alliance would have secured their position. Now they're re-evaluating their loyalties, and Ahmrose is circling.

**Sights:**

- **The Manatee Pastures:** Enormous enclosed kelp forests where manatees graze. The water is warm and green. Workers harvest kelp and tend the herds.
    
- **The Import Docks:** Surface ships unload cargo here—grain, textiles, surface technology, and luxury goods. Customs inspectors are notoriously bribable.
    
- **The Fish Markets:** Sprawling bazaars where fresh seafood, kelp bread, and imported delicacies are sold. The smell is overwhelming.
    
- **The Kelp Family Compound:** A sprawling, organic building grown from cultivated coral and kelp. The Kelp elders meet here to discuss the family's future.


**Sounds:** Rushing water, braying manatees, the shouts of dockworkers, the clatter of markets. **Smells:** Salt, kelp, fish, and the sweet rot of compost. **People:** Fishers, farmers, dockworkers, merchants, and the Kelp family's elders.

**DM Hooks:**

- A Kelp elder wants revenge for Reven's death. She suspects Ahmrose and will help the party if they can prove it.
    
- A shipment of surface weapons has gone missing. The Kelp family blames the Diamond Teeth; the Diamond Teeth blame the Blaang. The truth is more complicated.
    
- Lathos's research notes mention a specific star alignment that can only be observed from the surface isles. The Kelp family controls access.
 
## City-Wide Details

### Government

There is no central government. The four families rule their territories as they see fit. Disputes are settled by negotiation, bribery, or violence. The **Trenchspine Council** meets quarterly, but attendance is optional and decisions are non-binding. In practice, the families avoid open war because it would disrupt trade—but they constantly jockey for advantage.

### Law Enforcement

Each family has its own militia. In neutral areas (the Grand Market, the Trenchspine hub), a small **City Watch** patrols, funded by all four families. They're underpaid, overworked, and easily bribed.

### Religion

The gods are distant. Temples exist—Gris, god of death and safe travel, is popular among miners; a small temple to Leviathan exists in Kelp territory—but attendance is low. Project Lance has made divine magic unreliable, and many citizens have turned to alchemical remedies, superstition, or the cult of the Primordials. The gods are silent, and people are starting to notice.

### Economy

The metal trade is the backbone. Blaang mines it, Elwei manufactures it, Rockmund finances it, and Kelp feeds the workers. Everyone is connected, everyone is complicit, and everyone is looking for an edge.

### Crime

Smuggling, protection rackets, and black markets thrive. The Diamond Teeth are the largest criminal organization, but they operate openly as a "security company." Smaller gangs operate in the lower tiers. The **Sump Syndicate** controls the deep vents; the **Brass Fists** run protection in the Works.

### Culture

Victorian manners prevail: calling cards, formal introductions, and elaborate etiquette. Duels are technically illegal but still happen. Tea is imported and expensive; the poor drink kelp broth. Theater, opera, and music halls are popular in Rockmund territory. The lower tiers have their own entertainments: fighting pits, gambling dens, and storytellers.

### Current Events (Rumors the Party Hears)

- _"Zeke Blaang hasn't left his estate in weeks. They say he's lost his mind."_
    
- _"The Diamond Teeth are recruiting down in the Veins. Paying double. Something big is coming."_
    
- _"Kethy Rockmund is meeting with the Kelp elders. That can't be good for Elwei."_
    
- _"A surface ship brought something strange last week. A crate that hummed. The Kelp family took it straight to their compound."_
    
- _"The gods are silent. Haven't you noticed? No blessings, no omens. Just... nothing."_

## DM Notes: Making Ko'ar Tai Feel Alive

- **Three-dimensional movement:** NPCs swim, walk on ledges, and use lifts. Describe vertical spaces—chandeliers, drop-offs, current-chutes.
    
- **Sensory details:** Pressure changes, temperature gradients, the taste of minerals in the water, bioluminescent lighting, steam vents.
    
- **Class divide:** The wealthy live near the surface; the poor live near the vents. The middle tiers are the most diverse and the most tense.
    
- **Family loyalty:** Everyone has a family. Even the poor have cousins in the mines, aunts in the factories, or siblings in the Diamond Teeth. Betrayal is personal.
    
- **The gods are silent:** This should be a background dread. Temples are empty, prayers go unanswered, and people are looking for something else to believe in. The Primordials are an option—and Ahmrose knows it.
