The Sym is the largest region in Y'Valm by a small margin. The land transitions from South Arizona-esque rocky and hilled deserts populated with succulents and shrubs in the west, to dryer sparse regions in the northeast - both sections being bisected by a dense mass of rocky hills in the middle. The ethnic composition of the region is as follows: 60% Human, 15% Abyssinian Tabaxi, 15% High Elf, 10% Other.
