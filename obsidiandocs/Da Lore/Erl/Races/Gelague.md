

# Gelague

A Gelag is characterized by their stocky frame, light scales across the body, and neck gills. Gelague evolved on the extradimensional plane of water, but they make up a large portion of aquatic and even occasionally surface society.

Most Gelague are strong headed and stubborn. Their connection to the primeval world persuades them to rarely bother with political careers, preferring the company of a mill or plowed field. Tales of old describe how Gelague bested great aquatic threats like Krakens or Serpents, and many long to make a legend of their people again.

## Gelague Traits

- **Size**: Medium (about 5'6" - 5'9")

- **Age**: Gelague typically live 80-100 years

- **Speed**: 6m walking, swimming

- **Aquatic Adaptations**: You can breathe air along with water. You have Thermal sight out to 6m.

- **Primordial**: You can communicate simple ideas to any Beast, or Elemental. It can understand your words, though you have no special ability to understand it in return.

Optional Character Traits and Trivia

Gelague are the only mortals capable of seeing the color "Bome"
