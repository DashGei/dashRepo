# Yaldabaoth

_The Tower, Patron of Chaos, Demiurge _

Below the blasted mountains of Hell are the endless cyclopean labyrinths which spawn wave after wave of horrid Demons. Yaldabaoth spends most of his time watching them claw at each other, though occasionally he sends them to the surface to engage in more exciting battles against Devils.

**Domains:** Chaos, mutiny, disease, Demons  
**Worship Method:** Devotion

### Favor Criteria

- The party convinces enemies to turn on their leader.
- The party infiltrates their enemies before defeating them.
- The party turns a rigid system against itself.
- They behead their enemies in the name of Yaldabaoth.

### Disfavor Criteria

- The party aids Devils.
- The party lets arbitrary rules prevent them from achieving their goals.

### Boons

**Minor Boon (FP 4): Hatred.** The party adds 1d4 to saving throws made against spells of the Enchantment school.

**Boon (FP 7): Fortified Veins.** When a party member has the Poisoned condition they do not have disadvantage on ability checks.

**Major Boon (FP 15): Blanked.** Once per day, when a party member lands a critical hit on an enemy, that creature is affected by the _Crown of Madness_ spell until the start of their next turn.

### Woes

**Minor Woe (FP -3): Mind Blight.** While poisoned, a party member also has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws.

**Greater Woe (FP -5): Ailed.** Any party members with resistance or immunity to the Poisoned condition no longer have that resistance or immunity.

## Daughter Five

As foretold in *The Book of Baphul*, Daughter Five is the prophesized fifth child of Yaldabaoth, expected to bring forth a third and final era of mortal kind. No one knows when she will appear, where, or who will birth her, but she does have what are considered to be the most dangerous and vile of followers, even when compared to other demonic worshippers.

# Holy Texts, Sites, and Persons

*The Book of Baphul* is a 400 F.E scripture written by King Baphul of Prescia during a prolonged episode of psychosis which ended in the completion of the book, and the slaughter of ~32,000 Prescians. The book describes the circumstances surrounding the creation of Yaldabaoth's 4 children and the Daughter Five who is to arrive in the next (current) age. Scholars believe Yaldabaoth himself commanded Baphul to write the book as it also details the specifics Yaldabaothian Religion. This includes correct names for many demons, the procedures required for successful rituals, and ways in which to live life.
