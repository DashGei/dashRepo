# Cyrese

_The Hanged Man, Patron of Mercy_

Cyrese teaches that true strength is measured by compassion that is freely given, forgiveness that is sincerely offered, and burdens are to be carried without expectation of reward. His shrines are simple places where the sick are tended, prisoners are fed, and even enemies receive care.

**Domains:** Mercy, compassion, endurance, healing, forgiveness  
**Worship Method:** Ideals

### Favor Criteria

- The party spares a defeated enemy who no longer poses a threat.
- A party member willingly suffers hardship so another creature does not have to.
- The party provides meaningful aid to the sick, poor, or displaced without expecting payment.
- Two or more party members openly forgive one another for a past mistake.
- The party successfully ends a conflict through reconciliation instead of violence.

### Disfavor Criteria

- The party inflicts unnecessary suffering upon a helpless creature.
- They refuse aid to those in desperate need despite having the means to help.
- A party member seeks vengeance when justice or forgiveness would suffice.

### Boons

**Minor Boon (FP 4): Gentle Hands.** Once per long rest, when a party member restores hit points to another creature, that creature also gains temporary hit points equal to the healer's proficiency bonus.

**Boon (FP 7): Burdens Bared.** Once per long rest, when a creature within 5 feet takes damage, one party member may use their reaction to take half of that damage instead. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented.

**Major Boon (FP 15): Martyr's Grace.** Once per week (resetting on Same'ih), when a party member would be reduced to 0 hit points, another willing party member within 60 feet may choose to fall to 1 hit point instead. The original creature immediately regains hit points equal to half its maximum hit points.

### Woes

**Minor Woe (FP -3): Hardened Heart.** Once per day, the first time a party member restores hit points to another creature, reduce the healing by 1d4.

**Greater Woe (FP -5): Unworthy.** Whenever a party member regains hit points from a spell or feature cast by another creature, the healer takes PB x 1d2 force damage.
